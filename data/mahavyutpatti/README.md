# Mahāvyutpatti — DILA/DDBC TEI edition (banked source)

`mahavyutpatti.dila.tei.p5.xml.zip` — the TEI P5 digital edition of the
Mahāvyutpatti (bye brag rtog byed, early 9th c.) created by the Glossaries
Team at Dharma Drum Buddhist College / DILA, containing Sanskrit (IAST +
Devanāgarī), Tibetan (Wylie + Tibetan script) and Chinese for 9,379 entries
under the classical chapter headings, keyed by the standard Mahāvyutpatti
numbers.
