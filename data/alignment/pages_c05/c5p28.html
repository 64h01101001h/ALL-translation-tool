Segment 84 — dharmottara on the four attributes

Those hinges are banked at d=3 (phrase), not d=5: "if so" is a discourse connective for the whole concessive clause, not the dictionary equivalent of kyang or yod. Every Tibetan question is NEGATIVE (med, mi nus, ma yin, med) and every English question is POSITIVE ("has any purpose", "could ever achieve", "would want to", "there exists any relationship"), so mi, ma and both med have no exponent and are nulled — the negative-affix convention does not apply because GMR carried no negation into English.
