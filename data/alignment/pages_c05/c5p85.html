Geshe Michael expands freely around a spare Tibetan skeleton, and everything he supplies is deliberately left unwrapped — Here (twice), we will, we quoted first, We proceed in, steps, our, their, then, is the, and every article. AI NOTE —

An opponent's objection quoted and then turned into a consequence. The Tibetan frame is kha cig na re … zhes zer na, "if someone says…", and the objection closes on thal, the consequence particle: it would follow that. Geshe Michael spreads that single thal over two frames, "you must be suggesting that" and "you must be saying too that"; because they are not one contiguous string, thal is left unlinked.

Four content words are deliberately left with no English. bzang ngan is the pair good-or-bad, and Geshe Michael renders the clause as "there is no difference in their correctness at all": "correctness" names only the good pole, and "difference" is the head noun of his recasting rather than of the Tibetan. Every one of the four existing links for bzang ngan in the bank keeps the pair a pair — "good or bad", "for better or for worse", "fair or foul", "varying quality" — so neither half is banked here. rgyu mtshan de nyid kyi phyir na ("for that very reason") is recast as "Given the statements that have come above": there is no "reason" in the English and "Given" is the supplied hinge. gang du yang and sogs have no exponent.

The two med take the two negations Geshe Michael actually wrote: bzung du med do → "never" ("we should never again consider"), and the consequence's med par → "no" ("there is no difference"). That second med negates both coordinands and Geshe Michael voices it twice, at "no difference" and again at "could never state"; only the first is claimed, the second left unwrapped.

chos thams cad under negation is "anything". 'di yin 'di min, literally "this is, this is not", is aligned at the phrase layer (d=3, no members) to "one way or the other": the rendering is idiomatic for the disjunction as a whole and neither half has its own exponent, so it is kept out of the headword layer deliberately (precedent C05:37 gnas gang na → "in each of the eight", banked at d=3).

"non- Buddhists" with its broken hyphen is quoted exactly as it stands — the registered line-break class, and byte-identical in all three witnesses. bdag cag gi → "our" follows the licensed precedent at C05:182.

AI NOTE — A debate move plus its scriptural proof. The Tibetan opens with the REASON limb (...med pa'i phyir) of the consequence stated at C05:254, whose English there is the same string — "we should never again consider anything as being one way or the other" — so Geshe Michael's opening "And you must be suggesting all this" recaps 254's thal and has no Tibetan under it here. It is left unwrapped, and so are "you have claimed", "that none of the differences mentioned above exist" (what 'dod points back to), "again", "All", both "now", "only", "every", "Because", "it's", and the "for" of "go for refuge": supplied hinges and unpacking, rule 7.
Two phyir, two "for". The first closes the reason limb; the second (zhes gsungs pa'i phyir) sits at the very END of the Tibetan while its English "for as the verse says" sits BEFORE the verse. That crossing, and "never / consider / anything" against chos thams cad ... bzung ... med, are why this page needs an eng_order.
chos thams cad → "anything". Literally "all phenomena"; under the scope of the negation it surfaces as "anything" ("never consider anything" = "consider nothing among all phenomena"). The bank already renders it this way under a negation — "of anything in the universe", "no object in the universe". gang du yang is the MANNER of the negation, not its object, and is nulled.
'di yin 'di min is banded at d=3, not d=5. It is a coordinated pair of clauses ("it is this, it is not this"), not a word, and "one way or the other" renders the pair as a unit. Keeping it off the dictionary layer is deliberate: splitting it would slice a fixed English idiom, and minting it as a d=5 headword would bank a clause as a word.
yod is nulled. yod par thal is "it follows that they DO exist"; the only English in that slot is the pro-verb "do". The "exist" that does appear belongs to the negated antecedent Geshe Michael unpacked for 'dod na, so claiming it would attach this verb to the opposite proposition. bdag is nulled too: its exponent is locked inside the contraction "I've" and rule 8 does not permit cutting it out.
The verse (four lines, , , lineation; the capitals "All", "And", "No" are lineation, not sentence starts) is the refuge stanza. mi mnga' is kept flat rather than fused: mnga' → "has" (honorific "possess"), mi → "No" — Geshe Michael puts the negation on the noun where the Tibetan puts it on the verb, which is why those two cross.
