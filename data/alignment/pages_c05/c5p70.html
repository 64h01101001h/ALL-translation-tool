The wylie is one long topic phrase (rang gi ste … rten du gyur pa de la) which Geshe Michael broke into two English imperatives (“Consider now …; consider the energy …”). Neither “consider” has Tibetan under it — there is no chos can here — so both stay unwrapped, as do “as it exists”, “for this eye consciousness” (Geshe Michael repeating the referent, not a second mig gi rnam shes), “In truth”, “which” ×2, “something” ×2, “even”, “all”, “explanation” and every article.

Four deliberate nulls, each of them rule 10 rather than rule 7. skye ba is nulled because “producing” is its converse: the Tibetan says the consciousness ARISES FROM the moment of energy (gang zhig las skye ba), while the English makes the energy the producer. The bank carries skye ba 102 times — lives, develop, birth, arise, be born — and not once as a causative, so banking it here would key an intransitive verb to an active one. par med pa is nulled because the wylie reads par where the same sentence wrote bar eleven syllables earlier; “immediate” is left unwrapped rather than minting the headword par med pa from a misspelling. The correct bar med pa IS linked, on its first occurrence, to “immediately” — the word says “without interval”, and the before/after direction comes from the clause, so “before” is not taken. skad cig (“moment”, 16 links in the bank) occurs twice and has no exponent at all in this English. And rang gi is nulled: the bank's 31 rang gi links are 14 nulls and 17 possessives (its / its own / their / my), never a relative clause, and “related to it” is what Geshe Michael's clause does here, not what the word means.

The agentive gis is nulled under the measured practice recorded in the C05 campaign file: link the agentive only where a “by” governs the very agent it marks. There is no “by” in this English; “out of ignorance” is causal, and the bank nulls bare gis 72 times out of 81. The ablative las at rnam shes las tha dad pa IS linked, to “from”, because separation-from is the case's own work — while the first las is nulled, since the reversed voice leaves it nothing. kyi → “though” is the adversative, which Geshe Michael carries across the full stop into “In truth though”; the bank links kyi to “but” six times and the same adversative gyi to “though” twice. mig dbang is kept whole as a compound with its two members, because the bank already treats it as one unit four times out of four, once with this very English.
