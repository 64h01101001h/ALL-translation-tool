Segment 60 — why the accessories are called knowledge

GESHE MICHAEL'S ENGLISH (VERBATIM)
What is the actual process of causation here? Untainted wisdom springs from meditative wisdom, which comes from contemplative wisdom, which derives from the wisdom of learning--all dependent upon the wisdom with which one was born. Calling these accessories "knowledge" serves a specific purpose: the author is trying to tell us that they must be relied upon as methods, or causes, for achieving real knowledge.
