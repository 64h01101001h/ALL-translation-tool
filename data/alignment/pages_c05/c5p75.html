Segment 223 — the deed at its end, and three inventions to hold its energy

TIBETAN (WYLIE)
las byas pa'i skad cig gnyis par 'gags zin pa'i snga logs kyi las 'gag pa la mngon du phyogs pa'i tshe, las kyi nus pa gzhag par bya ba'i phyir kha cig kun gzhi rnam par shes pa rtog par byed la, kha cig bu lon gyi dpang rgya ste de'i yi ge dang 'dra ba'i las gnyis las don gzhan pa, chud mi za ba zhes pa ldan min 'du byed du gyur pa cig 'dod do, , kha cig ni las gnyis kyi thob pa zhes pa de gnyis las don gzhan du gyur pa'i ldan min zhig rtog par byed do
GESHE MICHAEL'S ENGLISH (VERBATIM)
Let's consider the deed or karma as it exists up to the point right after the moment in which the deed is completed; that is, let's consider the deed as it approaches its end. The energy of the deed has to be stored somewhere, and so some thinkers have invented the idea of some kind of foundation consciousness for it to stay. Others have said that there is something which is changing but neither mental nor physical, something they call "the fact of not just going away"; they say it is something that exists separately from the two types of deeds [virtue and non-virtue], and that it resembles the document written up for a loan that has to be repaid. AI NOTE — The question is where a deed's energy is kept once the deed itself has stopped, and the commentary walks three answers — each a device invented to hold the karmic charge: kun gzhi rnam par shes pa (the foundation consciousness), chud mi za ba (the "fact of not just going away", Sanskrit avipraṇāśa, likened to the written bond for a loan), and thob pa (the "hold", Sanskrit prāpti). Note that thob pa here is NOT the ordinary "attain / achieve" the bank records elsewhere: Geshe Michael names the technical possession-factor a "hold", and the dictionary's own glossary entry for the word carries "holds".
skad cig gnyis par is Geshe Michael's set idiom for "the moment after". Both analysts left gnyis par unlinked, reading "the point right after" as paraphrase; the corpus overrules them. The segment immediately before this one (C05:222) renders the same skad cig gnyis par as "the moment after", and C12:207 / C18:315 render it "the instant after" — so after is this word's own exponent, not a supplied hinge. Only the bare "after" is claimed; the intensifier "right" is left unwrapped.
Four Tibetan words are deliberately left with no English. dpang rgya (the witness-seal on a debt) and yi ge (the writing) are collapsed by Geshe Michael into one noun, "the document written up for a loan", and only yi ge is linked to it. 'gags zin pa (has already ceased) and snga logs (the side just before) have no exponent at all: "up to" is his frame of extent. phyir is nulled because his "and so" marks the RESULT where phyir marks the reason — a converse thrown up by his restructuring, not a gloss. In the last clause las gnyis ("of the two deeds") is dropped from the English name of the "hold", and "the two kinds of deeds themselves" renders the anaphoric de gnyis — only its gnyis is claimed, since "kinds of deeds" is supplied from the antecedent.
las appears seven times and carries three different jobs: the noun (deed) three times, the first member of the compound las gnyis, and twice the ablative particle rendered "from". The seventh, inside the nulled las gnyis kyi thob pa, is unrendered. Geshe Michael's opening "the deed or karma" is his own doublet naming of the term and is left bare: the two las of that sentence are linked to the two "deed"s that actually translate them.

Segment 224 — the mental seed, and the other three

TIBETAN (WYLIE)
kha cig ni las kyi bag chags kyis bsgos pa'i rnam shes kyi rgyun rtog par byed la, de'i phyir las 'gags kyang las kyis phyis yun ring po nas 'bras bu 'byin pa mi 'gal par 'dod de, las kyis kun gzhi la bag chags 'jog pas bag chags de las kyi 'bras bu yin la, de'i rigs 'dra brgyud pas mthar 'bras bu 'byin pas dang po'i las kyi 'bras bu brgyud pa las 'byung bar 'dod pa'o, , de bzhin du gzhan gsum la yang shes par bya'o
GESHE MICHAEL'S ENGLISH (VERBATIM)
Others finally have invented the idea of a stream of consciousness which is stained with the mental seed of the deed. This then is why, they say, that it is no contradiction for the deed to produce its consequence later, even after a very long time. The deed or karma plants a mental seed in the foundation consciousness, and so the mental seed is the result of the deed. This mental seed continues on in a stream of similar forms until eventually it produces the consequence. Thus, they say, the consequence of the original karma is something that is produced indirectly, via a medium. This same type of idea applies to the other three positions expressed.ni → ∅ (topic marker; unrendered)kyi → ∅ (genitive; the “of” is Geshe Michael’s English syntax)kyis → ∅ (instrumental; the “with” of “stained with” is governed by the English verb)kyi → ∅ (genitive; unrendered)la → ∅ (clause connective)las → ∅ (the ceased deed of `las 'gags kyang`; the whole concessive clause is unrendered)'gags → ∅ (has ceased; unrendered)kyang → ∅ (concessive “even though”; unrendered)kyis → ∅ (agentive; this sentence has no “by”)de → ∅ (clause connective)kyis → ∅ (agentive; Geshe Michael’s English is active, “plants”)pas → ∅ (causal; the “and so” is Geshe Michael’s connective)de → ∅ (demonstrative, flattened to “the”)kyi → ∅ (genitive; unrendered)pas → ∅ (causal; unrendered)pas → ∅ (causal; unrendered)kyi → ∅ (genitive; unrendered)las → ∅ (ablative “from/via”, not the noun karma)yang → ∅ (“also”; unrendered)shes par bya'o → ∅ (“should be understood”; recast as “applies”)
AI NOTE — The fourth and last of the non-Vaibhashika answers to how a deed long past can still throw its result: the mental-seed (bag chags) account. The deed deposits a seed in the kun gzhi, the foundation consciousness; that seed is itself called the deed's result, and it passes on through a continuum of like moments until the ripened consequence appears — so the original deed produces its consequence only through a medium. Geshe Michael then sends the same argument back over the other three positions (gzhan gsum).

Shape notes. The concessive las 'gags kyang, "even though the deed has ceased", has NO exponent in this English at all — an independent rendering of the same Tibetan at ILL:1881 carries it ("even though the karma itself has stopped"), which is how we know it is an abridgement and not a reading — so las, 'gags and kyang are nulled whole and the surviving "even" of "even after a very long time" is left unwrapped rather than tied to a clause Geshe Michael did not translate. brgyud is given twice in one sentence-pair: verbally ("continues on") where the seed is transmitted, and nominally ("medium") at brgyud pa las 'byung. "stream" stays with rgyun, which owns it 11 times in the landed bank; the "stream" of "a stream of similar forms" has no rgyun under it and is left unwrapped. kun gzhi takes "foundation consciousness" on the landed precedent at C05:213–214. The closing shes par bya'o ("should be understood") is nulled: "applies" is a recasting of the clause, not a rendering of a knowing-verb.

Segment 225 — who holds each of the four positions

TIBETAN (WYLIE)
de'i dang po ni sems tsam pa 'ga' zhig go, gnyis pa ni bye brag tu smra ba yin par spyan ras gzigs brtul zhugs kyis bshad de, kha che bye brag tu smra ba las gzhan pa zhig go, gsum pa yang bye brag tu smra ba'i nang tshan gcig go, bzhi pa la gsal kha ma byung yang mdzod 'grel gyi gnas dgu pa dang bstun na mdo sde pa dang kha che bye brag tu smra ba'i yang 'dod pa yin pa 'dra'o
GESHE MICHAEL'S ENGLISH (VERBATIM)
The first of these positions belongs to a certain group within the Mind-Only School. The second position is explained by Master Avalokitavrata to be that of the Detailist School--of a certain section other than the Kashmiri Detailists. The third position also belongs to a specific group within the Detailist School. AI NOTE —

The four positions listed above are now assigned to their holders. Read against the Tibetan, most of the connective tissue here is Geshe Michael's own: "positions", "belongs to", "within", "that of", "section", "Master", "completely", "an alternate" and "(Abhidharmakosha)" have no Tibetan under them and are left unwrapped. Sentences 1 and 3 end in the bare assertive go; there is no verb for "belongs" anywhere in the Tibetan.

Note the two occurrences of group. The first is Geshe Michael's supplied head noun for the quantifier 'ga' zhig ("some / a certain number") and carries no link; the second does have a word under it, nang tshan, a subdivision inside a larger body. The numeral gcig after it is left unwrapped.

mdzod is the abbreviated title of the Treasure House of Knowledge; only Treasure House is linked, since of Knowledge renders chos mngon pa, which is not in this line. gnas dgu pa is its ninth chapter, the Pudgala-vinishchaya: the root verses have eight chapters and the ninth exists only in the autocommentary, which is exactly what the English says. The count is right.
