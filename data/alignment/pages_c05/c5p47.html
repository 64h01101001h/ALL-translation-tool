The root verse's question and answer (iv.45-8) on whether the ethical character of the starting motivation (rab tu 'jug byed, the initiating impulse) fixes that of the continuing motivation (rjes su 'jug byed). Each compound occurs twice, once in the question and once in the answer; GMR renders them as "starting types of motivation" / "the starting types" and "continuing types of motivation" / "types of continuation". GMR's four clauses are a permutation matrix over two Tibetan terms — rab tu 'jug byed (the starting/impelling motivation) and rjes su 'jug byed (the continuing one) — each crossed with dge ba (virtue) or lung ma bstan (ethically neutral). GMR keeps the pairs lexically constant (“starting” and “continuing” four times each, so the four Tibetan occurrences of each resolve one-to-one) while varying the head noun he supplies: “types of motivation”, “motivations”, “motivation” have no Tibetan under them. The Tibetan has no word for motivation anywhere in this segment; the two terms are bare nominalised verbs (“that which starts” / “that which follows on”) whose head noun (kun slong) is understood from the running discussion, so “starting” and “continuing” are the honest extent of the link.
