AI NOTE — A debate consequence (thal … phyir) put to the opponent: if five different limbs doing five different things meant five different agents, then one person would never be performing five actions. Geshe Michael renders yan lag twice, and differently each time — limbs where he spells the five out ("all four of his limbs, and his head"), protuberances where he names them as a group. Both are in the master's own equivalents for yan lag, and C05:283 carries the second one again (yan lag lnga po de → "The five protuberances"). Only the head nouns are spanned: "all four of", "and his head" and "of his body" have no Tibetan under them. The first mi 'dra ba and the first lngas are nulled, because the opening clause never calls the limbs different and never says "five" over them — that single pair of nulls is what lines the remaining four mi 'dra ba up with the four "different" and the remaining lnga/lngas with the four "five". "According to you" is left unwrapped as supplied debate frame. thal is the only consequence marker present, but C05:257 — the same … par thal, … pa'i phyir shape — nulls thal and leaves the modal unwrapped, and C05:281 shows Geshe Michael getting "according to you" from khyod ltar na, which is absent here. The instrumental force carried by "is using", and the "would" of "wouldn't", are likewise unwrapped.
