Segment 343 — dying before or together, no actual stage

TIBETAN (WYLIE)
snga dang mnyam du shi ba la, ,dngos med lus gzhan skyes phyer ro, ,dmag la sogs par don gcig phyir, ,thams cad byed pa po bzhin ldan
GESHE MICHAEL'S ENGLISH (VERBATIM)
There is no actual stage for those who die Before or together, for another body's been born. Because their goal's the same in a war or whatever, All possess it as much as the one who commits it.sentence-final terminative; unrendered
AI NOTE — Root verse of the Treasure House of Knowledge IV, quoted inside Gendun Drup's commentary and unpacked in prose at C05:344–346. The four , , breaks are verse lineation, which is why Before, Because and All carry capitals mid-sentence — lineation, not sentence starts.

The verse abbreviates: dngos is not “actual stage”. The commentary at C05:344 spells the term out as las lam gyi dngos gzhi, “the actual ‘commission’ stage of a full path of action”, so the verse's bare dngos is dngos gzhi shortened for the metre. Only actual is banked; stage is left unwrapped, because the word that means stage (gzhi) is not in this line and dngos = “actual stage” would put half of a two-word technical term into the dictionary layer. Both analysts reached this independently, and the generator accepts the wider span — it was checked: this is a Rule 10 ruling, not a mechanical one.

A spelling slip, and why it sits at d=6. Line 2 ends skyes phyer ro in the wylie and SKYES PHYER RO in the ACIP, the source of record. Line 3 of this same segment spells the particle correctly (don gcig phyir), and the same verse line quoted again inside this course at C05:372 reads skyes phyir ro. The text is kept verbatim and the slip is filed as an erratum, never corrected on the page. Because phyer is not a Tibetan word — it is absent from the 105,634-entry master and appears in exactly two corpus rows, this one and its C16 re-ingest — the span is banked at d=6, the particle layer, which the dictionary harvest does not read. The correctly spelled phyir in line 3 sits at d=5, the bank's dominant depth for it (216 d=5 links against 84 d=6). The asymmetry is deliberate: record what the document says without minting a headword that does not exist.

Two causal readings, two fors. la is the dative on shi ba (“for those who die”); phyer is causal phyir rendered “for” = because. Both occurrences of for are claimed, so the two resolve by order.

bzhin at the particle layer. The postposition means “like / just as”; Geshe Michael's as much as turns the likeness into a degree. Nothing else in the line carries the comparison, and the commentary at C05:346 has the same rendering (byed pa po bzhin du → “just as much as the one who commits it”), so it is banked — but at d=6, where a loose comparative cannot become a headword.

Left unwrapped (Rule 7): There is (the existential frame over med), stage, those who and the one who (supplied relative frames), their, 's been, in a, and both its — the verse has no pronoun for their and no object for it. byed pa po takes Geshe Michael's own phrase one who commits without the article; the agentive po is left unclaimed rather than invent one who for it.

Segment 345 — no commission when the killer dies first or together

TIBETAN (WYLIE)
de la de 'byung ba med de, gsad bya ma shi bar de mi 'byung , gsad bya dang mnyam du shi na lus gzhan skyes shing , des pha rol po gsod pa'i sbyor ba ma byas pa'i phyir
GESHE MICHAEL'S ENGLISH (VERBATIM)
The answer is that there is no occurrence of this stage for such a person. In the first place, the stage cannot occur before the victim dies; in the second place, where the murderer dies at the same time as his victim, another body of the killer has been born. There is no actual commission because the body never went through the stage of undertaking this act towards the victim in question.de (demonstrative; "such a person" is Geshe Michael's supplied noun) → ∅de (clause connective of med de) → ∅ma (negation of the ma…bar idiom; "before" rests on the whole construction) → ∅bar (terminative; the layer has 68 bar nulls at d=6 and no "before") → ∅de ("the stage" is Geshe Michael's supplied noun) → ∅mi (negation only; the modal of "cannot" is supplied and cannot be split off) → ∅dang (comitative governed by mnyam du) → ∅na (conditional; "where" is Geshe Michael's frame) → ∅shing (connective; no "and" in the English) → ∅des (agentive demonstrative; no "by" governs it in the English) → ∅pha rol po ("the other party"; "victim" is gsad bya, see note) → ∅gsod pa ("killing"; "this act" is a placeholder noun) → ∅byas ("went through" is Geshe Michael's phrasal verb) → ∅
AI NOTE — Vasubandhu's answer to the question posed at C05:344: a murderer who dies before the victim, or at the same moment, never reaches the actual commission (dngos gzhi) stage of the path of action. The Tibetan gives two reasons and then the ground for the second; Geshe Michael Roach supplies the scaffolding that makes them read as English argument. Left deliberately unwrapped (rule 7 — no Tibetan stands under it): “The answer is that” (Geshe Michael Roach's reply-frame for the zhe na of 344), “In the first place” / “in the second place”, “such a person”, “the murderer” (shi na has no expressed subject), “of the killer” (the Tibetan is only lus gzhan skyes, “another body is born”), “There is no actual commission” (the consequent phyir leaves implicit), “towards” and “in question”. ma … bar. gsad bya ma shi bar is the negative terminative, “while the victim has not died”; the English “before” is carried by the negation and the terminative together, so neither ma nor bar is given it alone — the bank holds 94 bar links, 68 of them null at d=6, and not one on “before”. mi is nulled. Its only candidate is the “not” fused inside “cannot”, which is outside the four permitted negative-affix forms, and the modal “can” is Geshe Michael Roach's, not the particle's; this is the C05:170 shape, where “do not” was split and the supplied auxiliary left unwrapped. pha rol po is nulled. The sentence uses gsad bya and pha rol po of the same man and the English says “victim” for both, but pha rol po is “the other party” — rendered “someone else” at C05:344 and “someone from the other side” at C05:346 — while gsad bya is the word that means victim. Banking both would teach that the two are synonyms. gsod pa and byas are nulled for the same reason: “this act” is a placeholder and “went through” is a phrasal verb, with ma taking the negation as “never”.
