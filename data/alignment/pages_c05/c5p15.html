Segment 43 — the third point: from the words of the work itself

TIBETAN (WYLIE)
gsum pa ni gzhung gi tshig don las shes bar 'gyur ro
GESHE MICHAEL'S ENGLISH (VERBATIM)

AI NOTE —

gsum pa ni opens the third of an enumerated set; only “third” has Tibetan under it. GMR's long stretch “point we have promised, a description of the subject matter presented in each of the eight chapters” is his supplied reminder of what the third promised topic was and is left unwrapped, as are “the”, “Master's” (no slob dpon in the line) and “itself” (no rang/nyid).

gzhung is the treatise, “work”. tshig don is the standard pair words-and-meaning; GMR renders only “words”, so tshig takes it and don is nulled rather than pinned to the earlier “subject matter”. Note the crossing: the genitive runs work-of-words in Tibetan, “words of the … work” in English. las is the ablative “from”. shes bar 'gyur ro is the future/resultative verb phrase “will be understood”, with shes carrying “understood”.

Segment 45 — knowledge is unstained wisdom

TIBETAN (WYLIE)
chos mngon shes rab dri med rjes 'brang bcas
GESHE MICHAEL'S ENGLISH (VERBATIM)
Knowledge is unstained wisdom, and its accessories."together with"; GMR carries the joining by the conjunction "and", left unwrapped as supplied English (the C05:47 parallel renders dang bcas pa the same way, where the "and" sits on dang)
AI NOTE — Root verse of the Treasure House of Knowledge (Abhidharmakosha I.2ab), quoted in Gendun Drup's commentary as the definition of chos mngon pa. chos mngon ("higher dharma", Abhidharma) is GMR's Knowledge throughout C05. The verse is a bare nominal string: shes rab wisdom is the head, dri med unstained its modifier (head-then-modifier in Tibetan, reversed in English, so the English order crosses). dri med is split under the negative-affix convention: dri "stain" carries stained, med the negation un. rjes 'brang (lit. "what follows after") is GMR's accessories, the attendant factors, as in C05:47. The copula is, the possessive its and the comma are GMR-supplied. bcas is the comitative "together with"; GMR's conjunction and does its joining work but is left unwrapped rather than banked on the particle.
