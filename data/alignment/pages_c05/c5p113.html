AI NOTE — Root verse [iv.283–4] of the Treasure House of Knowledge, quoted ahead of the commentary that unpacks it at C05:340–342. The verse names the object (gzhi) of each of the four divisions of non-virtue: living beings, enjoyments, names-and-forms, and names. Every equivalent here is confirmed by Geshe Michael's own English in that commentary — gzhi “object(s)” at C05:340, 341 and 342; longs spyod “enjoyments” at 341; ming dang gzugs “names and forms” at 342 (cha gsum pa'i gzhi ni ming dang gzugs yin te → “The objects of the third division are names and forms”). ming dang gzugs is taken as a d=5 compound (nāmarūpa) with ming and gzugs as members, rather than three flat spans; the dang inside it is left unlisted rather than given the compound's own “and”. The verse carries ONE finite copula, yin no, at the very end, and the English carries ONE verb, “consist of”, in its first line; they are linked and the crossing is declared in eng_order, on the warrant of C05:340, where the same construction cha dang po'i gzhi ni sems can yin te is rendered “The object of the non-virtues of the first division consists of living beings”. Four particles are nulled: both ni, the list dang after longs spyod (Geshe Michael writes a comma and a line break, not “and”), and the final no. “The”, “and then” and “as well” are Geshe Michael's own connective apparatus for the fourth item and carry no Tibetan; they stay unwrapped. The mid-sentence capital in “Names” is verse lineation, kept byte-for-byte.
