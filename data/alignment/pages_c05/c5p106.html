AI NOTE — Gendun Drup opens the exposition of the four deeds of Treasure House IV.237–40 — black, white, both, unstained — by defining the first. The Tibetan states its reasons first and its conclusions last; Geshe Michael reverses every one of them, so the English side crosses at four places and the page carries an explicit eng_order. Four gnag, four “black”, one to one, and each is spanned as the bare word gnag: two of them stand in gnag pa'i, where the nominaliser and genitive have no exponent of their own. Both causal pas particles are linked — the first to “in that”, the second to “for” — because each English subordinator introduces exactly the clause its particle closes. The closing phyir is nulled: there is only one “for” and it belongs to pas. rnam smin is read twice, differently: the noun in “an undesirable result”, then the predicate “ripens” under yang. “ripens forth” is left unwrapped and 'byin is nulled — it means bring forth, not ripen. ngo bo likewise appears twice: nulled the first time, where the question “what is their nature?” is recast as “how do we describe”, and linked to “nature” alone the second time, with rang gi carrying “its” and the intensifier “very” left unwrapped — Geshe Michael writes “its basic nature” in the same slot two rows later, so the adjective there is his and varies. Also unwrapped as supplied English: “four types of” (the Tibetan says only las de dag; the count is correct and comes from the root verse), “a type of”, “represents”, “do we describe”, and each “something”.

Segment 318 — white deeds: virtue of the form realm

TIBETAN (WYLIE)
gzugs su gtogs pa'i dge ba ni, dkar la rnam par smin pa dkar ba'i las yin te, rang gi ngo bo rgyud gcig la nyon mongs pa dang ma 'dres, rnam smin yid du 'ong ba 'byin zhing rgyud gcig la sdug bsngal dang ma 'dres ba'i phyir
GESHE MICHAEL'S ENGLISH (VERBATIM)
Virtue taken in by the realm of form represents a type of deed which is white and also ripens into something white. This is first of all because of its basic nature: it is unmixed with afflicted types of things, within the mental stream in question. And it ripens forth into a desirable result; with the one mental stream, it is not mixed together with any suffering.su → ∅ (terminative; its force is absorbed into the phrasal verb "taken in by")ni → ∅ (topic marker; unrendered)la → ∅ (connective; unrendered)yin te → ∅ (copula + connective; "represents" is Geshe Michael's supplied hinge verb, not a rendering of yin)gcig → ∅ ("in question" is not a rendering of the numeral; the second gcig IS "one", see w19)la → ∅ (locative; "within" is supplied)dang → ∅ (comitative; "with" occurs three times against two dang, so rule 6 cannot resolve it)'byin → ∅ (bring forth / produce; the only English left for it is the "forth" of Geshe Michael's own verb "ripens forth", and an adverb is not an exponent of the verb)zhing → ∅ (the English "And" opens a sentence earlier than zhing sits; rendered here by the semicolon)la → ∅ (locative; unrendered)dang → ∅ (comitative; unequal counts, see p5)
AI NOTE — The second of the four deeds of Abhidharmakosha IV: virtue belonging to the form realm is the white deed — white in nature and white in ripening. Geshe Michael renders one Tibetan sentence as three English ones, and the English crosses the Tibetan repeatedly: dge ba follows its modifier gzugs su gtogs pa'i in Tibetan but opens the English, and phyir, dead last in Tibetan, surfaces as "because" in the middle. Several stretches of his English have no Tibetan under them and are left unwrapped: "the realm of" (the wylie has only gzugs, form — no khams), "types of things" after "afflicted" (no chos in the wylie), "result" in "a desirable result" (no 'bras bu here; C05:319 does have it), and "represents", "a type of", "This is first of all", "in question", "something" and "any", which are his supplied frame. gcig is banked twice with different answers on purpose: at its second occurrence Geshe Michael wrote "the one mental stream" and it is linked; at its first he wrote "the mental stream in question", which is not a rendering of one, so it is nulled rather than stretched. ma 'dres is banked whole at both occurrences — "unmixed", with the negation ma owning the prefix un and 'dres owning mixed, and "not mixed", with the same two members. 'byin (bring forth) is nulled: the only English left for it is the "forth" of his own verb "ripens forth". Both dang are nulled because "with" occurs three times against two dang, and unequal counts do not resolve.
