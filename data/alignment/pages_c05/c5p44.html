Segment 130 — the three root non-virtues by nature

TIBETAN (WYLIE)
mi dge ba'i rtsa ba gsum dang ngo tsha med pa dang khrel med chos can, ngo bo nyid kyis mi dge ba yin te, gzhan la ma ltos par bdag nyid kyis mi dge ba yin pa'i phyir, mi 'phrod pa'i sman bzhin

AI NOTE —

The subject line is a three-member list under one chos can: mi dge ba'i rtsa ba gsum, ngo tsha med pa, khrel med. GMR moves chos can to the front as "Next consider" (only "consider" is banked, per the C04 convention; "Next" and the three "They are" frames are his and have no Tibetan under them). The first mi dge ba takes "non-virtues" (the plurality is gsum's, but the singular string is not available at a word boundary there); the two later ones take "non-virtue".

ngo tsha med pa is banked with its members ngo tsha "shame" and the negation med carrying the suffix "less" (negative-affix convention). khrel med "lack of a conscience" carries one member, khrel "conscience"; its med has no separable exponent ("lack of" runs in the reverse order) and is not split out. The second dang is left null: "and" occurs twice in the English, once inside GMR's idiom "in and of themselves".

Two instrumentals, two rulings: ngo bo nyid kyis is an instrumental of manner and keeps "by"; bdag nyid kyis is absorbed into "in and of themselves", so only bdag nyid is banked. Clause-final phyir carries clause-initial "for"; te is null. gzhan la ma ltos par gives "without relying" and "else"; "on anything" is GMR's filler.

Rule 10. The closing simile mi 'phrod pa'i sman bzhin reads literally "like medicine that does not agree with one" ('phrod pa: to suit, agree with, be compatible). GMR renders it "like poisonous herbs". "Poisonous" is his compression of the image, not an equivalent of mi 'phrod pa, and is left unwrapped; only sman "herbs" and bzhin "like" are banked.
