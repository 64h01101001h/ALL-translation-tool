AI NOTE — Root verse of the Treasure House of Knowledge (Abhidharmakosha IV) on the fields in which a deed ripens at once; the commentary unpacks it line by line at C05:186–188. Geshe Michael inverts the Tibetan wholesale. The Tibetan names the recipients first (lines 1–2) and states the help-or-harm and its result last (lines 3–4); the English states the help-or-harm and the result first and appends the list after a colon. 'gog, byams, mthong and dgra bcom stand for 'gog pa (cessation), byams pa tshad med (immeasurable love), mthong lam (the path of seeing) and dgra bcom pa (the Destroyer of the Foe, the arhat) — all four spelled out in the commentary at 186–188. The arhat term is not banked whole on "destroying": dgra is nulled and only bcom (destroy, conquer) is linked, because C05:188 renders the same compound in full as "the result of destroying the enemy", where "destroying" belongs to bcom and "the enemy" to the dgra this line leaves unrendered. GESHE MICHAEL'S ENGLISH (VERBATIM)
One may ask about the features that make an object special. Help or harm which you perform towards anyone who has just come out of cessation leads to a quick experience of a result, for it is almost as if such a person has just come back from nirvana. Help and harm too towards anyone who has just come out of a meditation on immeasurable love or a state of one-pointed concentration where he has no affliction at all also leads to the speedy experience of a result. This is because such persons are helping every living being; they are involved with a willingness to accept the ultimate personal responsibility, free of any affliction; and they are totally imbued with an immeasurable kind of merit, very sharp and clear.gi → ∅ (genitive; unrendered)ci lta bu → ∅ ('of what sort'; absorbed into the question frame)las → ∅ (ablative; the 'of' of 'come out of' is a hinge)pas → ∅ (agentive, spanned whole per rule 9; 'which you' is supplied)te → ∅ (connective; unrendered)de → ∅ ('that one'; the English recasts it as an expletive 'it' plus a supplied 'such a person', and neither is a safe exponent)dang → ∅ (comparative marker with 'dra ba; unrendered)las → ∅ (ablative; second occurrence)byas → ∅ ('performed'; the parallel English clause supplies no second 'perform')pas → ∅ (agentive, spanned whole per rule 9)te → ∅ (connective; unrendered)la → ∅ (la-don; unrendered here)dang → ∅ (list coordinator; the English breaks the list with a semicolon)zhing → ∅ (gerundive connective)pas → ∅ (agentive, spanned whole per rule 9)
