Segment 71 — the purpose of explaining knowledge

TIBETAN (WYLIE)
[i.9-12] chos mngon pa bshad pa la dgos pa yod de, chos rnams rab tu rnam 'byed kyi shes rab med par nyon mongs pa rnams nye bar zhi bar bya ba'i thabs med la, nyon mongs pas kyang 'jig rten pa rnams srid pa'i rgya mcho 'dir 'khyam ste, de bas na slob ma'i rgyud la chos rab tu rnam 'byed kyi shes rab bskyed pa'i dgos pa yod pa de'i phyir
GESHE MICHAEL'S ENGLISH (VERBATIM)
Giving an explanation of knowledge has the following purpose. Without wisdom which analyzes every phenomenon in an ultimate way, there is no way of putting to rest all that the mental afflictions imply. And this affliction is what keeps those of the world adrift here in the ocean of life. It is therefore necessary to plant, in students' minds, wisdom which can make this ultimate analysis of phenomena. The commentary's purpose-statement for the whole Treasure House of Knowledge, paraphrasing root verse i.9-12 (quoted at C05:70). The Tibetan is one long reason-statement (dgos pa yod de … dgos pa yod pa de'i phyir); GMR breaks it into five English sentences.
