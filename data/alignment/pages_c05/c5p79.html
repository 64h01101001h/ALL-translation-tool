AI NOTE — Gendun Drup quotes Nāgārjuna's Sixty Verses on Reasoning (rigs pa drug cu pa), two quatrains, closed by zhes gsungs te. Geshe Michael fronts the quotative ("Now the … says") and renders the root lines as capitalised English lines; the mid-sentence capitals are verse lineation, not sentence starts.

The first quatrain is already banked at C05:230, where the same four Tibetan lines carry almost the same English. This page follows that landed reading throughout: rkyen, rdzogs pa'i, de yi phyir na, can du, yin and the first zhes are nulled there and are nulled here; gang phyir → "For", cis → "why", mi 'thad → "wrong" and the rigs pa / drug cu members are banked there and are banked here.

Four words are deliberately left unbanked. (1) rkyen is 'condition'; all 22 banked rkyen links read condition/conditions/factor/circumstance/result and none reads 'cause'. In Abhidharma rkyen (pratyaya) is precisely not rgyu (hetu), and "ignorance causes" recasts the whole rkyen can du phrase as a verb, so it stays unwrapped. (2) kun brtags is parikalpita, the conceptually imputed. The second "conceptions" is the only English left for it, but the one banked kun brtags link reads 'consciously believe', and Geshe Michael's own rendering of this identical Tibetan line elsewhere (ILL:2676, SVN:1076) reads "Imaginations". Banking "conceptions" on it would make kun brtags a synonym of rnam rtog — which is banked on the first "conceptions" here — and that is false. (3) gsal is 'become clear / come to light'; "could never Disappear" runs the other way, and the same witnesses render this line "Were brought to light". (4) The second las is ablative 'from'; "Despite" is concessive, a different relation.

AI NOTE — RECONCILED from a tibetan-first and an english-first proposal. The Tibetan closes with zhes pa'i don no, “that is the meaning of what was said”, and Geshe Michael puts that sentence FIRST in English, so the English order crosses the segment end-to-end; eng_order carries it.

The clause with no exponent. gsal du 'gro dgos kyi — “[they] would have to go on becoming manifest, but” — reaches this English only as the modal dgos → “should” and the adversative kyi → “although”. gsal and 'gro, the verb of the clause, have no exponent at all: Geshe Michael's “although they should have” attaches the obligation to DISAPPEARING, which is the opposite of what the Tibetan requires. Both are left null rather than stretched onto “disappear” or “should have”; an erratum is filed. An independent rendering of the identical Tibetan at ILL:2677 reads “the truth would have to be brought to light” — obligation on the manifesting, as the Tibetan has it.

Left unwrapped as supplied English (rule 7): “things”, “some independent” before “reality” (gnas lugs is the way things are; “independent” is Geshe Michael's gloss and has no Tibetan under it), “as follows”, “were so, then”, both “then”s, “you”, “finally”, “your”, “states of mind” after “mistaken”, the elliptical “have” of “should have”, and every article.

AI NOTE — Vasubandhu's line that the mind is the main thing, with Gendun Drup's gloss: the world of beings and the world of vessels alike are produced by karma those beings themselves accumulated. Geshe Michael expands sems can gyi 'jig rten and snod kyi 'jig rten into his "in the form of …" formula, so 'jig rten keeps "world"/"worlds" while sems can and snod take the head of the relative clause — "those who live" and "places". The trailing "in it" and "where these beings live" are left unwrapped: they point at the world, which is outside the span. thun mang ba'i is left unlinked. The wylie and the ACIP of record both misspell thun mong ba'i, and the independent witness ILL:2680 carries this same sentence with thun mong ba'i las kyis and renders it "the communal karma they have accumulated"; a d=5 link on "collective" would make the misspelling a dictionary headword, so "collective" stays unwrapped and the erratum carries the information (the E-169 precedent). shin tu is nulled for the same kind of reason in miniature: it is the degree word "extremely", and "vast" is a size adjective. Of the four agentive/instrumental particles only two are linked — kyis on rang gi sems to "with", and kyis on thun mang ba'i las to "by" — because in those two places the English preposition governs exactly the constituent the particle marks; pas and the kyis on sems nyid are nulled because the available "through"s and "by"s do not divide one-to-one.
