GESHE MICHAEL'S ENGLISH (VERBATIM)
The 400 Verses says as well: Without conceptions, desire and such Are nothing that can even exist; Why then would anyone with a brain Believe in reality and conception? The commentary to the work states that-- Things that can't even exist in the absence of conceptions are, beyond any manner of doubt, absolutely things that cannot exist through any essence of their own: they are like a coil of rope you label with "snake." The expression "reality" here refers to something that could exist through its own essence.
