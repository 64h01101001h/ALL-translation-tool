AI NOTE —

Vasubandhu / Gendun Drup, the glass-of-water debate. The opponent has just argued (C05:302) that the human's water is real because he can wash with it; here he answers the consequence by claiming mi mtshungs — the two cases are not parallel, because what the craving spirit undergoes is only rang gi blos rlom pa tsam, a mere fabrication of his own mind. The reply turns the same words back on him: then nothing happens to the human either.

Shape. Flat — d=5 words and d=6 particles in wylie order, no clause layer, with one compound opened to members (kho na re → "Someone may respond", kho / na re). That member analysis is not a novelty here: landed C05:283 carries the byte-identical opening frame byas pa la kho na re and treats it span for span the same way — byas pa nulled, la → "to", kho na re opened to kho "someone" and na re "responds", zer na nulled. The English inverts the Tibetan at four points (the agent lands before the la it follows; tsam before the noun it measures; yang's "either" eight words after med's "n't"; the second phyir opens the clause its Tibetan closes), and four strings repeat ("happens", "imagination", "nothing more than", "to"), so eng_order is load-bearing.

Where the two readings differed. des na is given only "then", not "Well then": the bank carries "Well then" five times and every one of them is 'o na, never des na, and landed C05:284 already ruled Geshe Michael's "Well" a discourse hinge with no Tibetan under it. de ltar → "all this" is kept in the first clause and nulled in the second, because that asymmetry is Geshe Michael's: "all this" stands in the master's own glossary list for de ltar, while in the second clause he writes only the relative "what", which is not this phrase. mi mtshungs is taken as mi "not" plus mtshungs "same" rather than as one compound glossed "not the same", which would have swallowed an article no Tibetan supports.

Rule 10, nulled rather than banked. byas pa is not "this argument" — the noun is supplied, and C05:304's identical frame gets only "to this". blos keeps its null: the master glosses it "intent" alone, and the noun the English shows is rlom pa. The first phyir is nulled because its sentence states no reason word at all. The second de (de mi rang gi blos) is nulled although it is plainly the subject: its only English exponent is the "it" inside the contraction "it's", and a word may not be sliced.

Banked on precedent. rlom pa → "imagination" is the master's own equivalent (imagined, mistaken, imagination) and is new to this layer. tsam → "nothing more than" has four landed links and is in the master list. yang → "either" is in the master list for yang and is landed twice on its twin kyang. yi dvags → "craving spirit" is landed 22 times.
