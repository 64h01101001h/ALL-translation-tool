One Tibetan objection sentence: mthong spang gis … mi nus na (the protasis restating the earlier thesis), mdo las … bshad pa (the scriptural counter-testimony), and dang 'gal lo zhe na (the contradiction charge). GMR breaks it into three English sentences and supplies the debate frame — “One may begin as follows”, “You have stated above that”, “though”, “something which is”, “lead to”, “Isn't there … here” — none of which has Tibetan under it, so all of it is left unwrapped.
