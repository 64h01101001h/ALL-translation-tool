Geshe Michael turns it into two English sentences and supplies all the framing, which is left deliberately UNWRAPPED under rule 7: “This brings us to our”, “point”, “a detailed discussion of”, “We present”, “first”, “the way these paths are”, “and finally”, “of the expression”. None of it has Tibetan under it.

The English crosses the Tibetan once, at the third item: the wylie is las lam gyi sgra don (possessor first) and the English is “the literal meaning of the expression ‘a path of action’” (possessed first), so eng_order places sgra don before the second las lam.

Contrast C05:333, where yid kyi gsum is “the three misdeeds of thought” and the “of” governs the very noun yid marks, so it was linked. la and the final no are nulled as framing and declarative closer.

AI NOTE — Root verse of the Treasure House, six seven-syllable lines under the double-shad lineation; the mid-sentence capitals (Brought, Stealing) are line beginnings, not sentence starts. Each line names a group of non-virtues and the affliction that carries them through to completion — zhe sdang dislike, chags pa desire, gti mug ignorance — and the closing line assigns the remaining deeds to all three at once. The page follows the shape already landed at C05:328, which is the prose statement of this very verse (las lam thams cad rtsa ba gsum gsum gyis mthar phyin par byed pa → “the various paths of action are each brought to completion by these three roots”): there mthar phyin carries “completion”, byed pa carries “brought”, the terminative par is nulled, and the agentive gyis is linked to “by”. The same four decisions are taken here. The four agentives (gis, yis, gis, gyis) each govern exactly the agent their “by” governs in the English; the topic particle ni is nulled twice. Everything Geshe Michael supplied is left UNWRAPPED: the passive auxiliary are (twice), the hinges to their / to / as, the article The, the second and (there is no second dang under it), and of things, which has no Tibetan at all — gti mug gis is simply “by ignorance”. In the last line lhag ma is the rest, the remaining deeds, while gsum gyis is the instrument, “by three”, the three afflictions together; the master treats lhag ma gsum as one headword but Geshe Michael's English separates them, so the page follows his reading. ma byin lan is quoted exactly as this row has it, per the verbatim rule — but it is a spelling defect, and it is filed as one rather than cleared. The commentary's own prose restatement of this verse, two rows later at C05:337, reads brnab sems ma byin len in the same three-item list, and ma byin len, taking what is not given, is the word; lan is not. The master does carry a ma byin lan headword, but its recorded corpus basis is these two rows alone — this one and its byte-identical C16 re-ingest — so it is a scaffold built from the defect, not independent attestation of it. Rule 1 keeps the span as written; the errata register carries the correction.
