AI NOTE —

A debate consequence in the standard shape: the consequence first (de mi 'thad par thal, “it follows that that is untenable”), then the reason clause closed by pa'i phyir. Geshe Michael inverts that order, so phyir — the last word of the Tibetan — surfaces as for near the front of the English, and the English side crosses throughout; the order is given explicitly.

Though is not a rendering of thal. The corpus holds 42 rows with “is though”, among them C08:149 (“There is though no contradiction”) over skyon med de, where no thal occurs at all. It is Geshe Michael's habitual concessive, so thal carries no span.

Much of this English has no Tibetan under it and is left unwrapped. “Your reasoning here” is the referent supplied for a bare de. “You have expressed” is a recast of de ltar. “Shows that” stands where the Tibetan has skyon yin pa'i, “is the fault of”. “The whole … expressed above” stands where phyogs snga smra ba po'i, “of the one who proposes the earlier position”, was collapsed: phyogs snga is the earlier SIDE of a debate, not a place-reference, so it is left null rather than linked to above.

Master is an honorific Geshe Michael supplies; this Tibetan carries no slob dpon. The previous segment, C05:275, renders dpal ldan chos kyi grags pa “the glorious Dharmakirti”, which shows an honorific is translated when one is present. The genitive 'i is taken with the name because the English possessive is there to answer it. lung don is confirmed by the next segment, C05:277, where the same chos kyi grags pa'i lung don is rendered “the meaning of Master Dharmakirti's statement”.

khong du ma chud pa is kept whole. The idiom lives in khong du … chud pa — the bank's one landed link reads khong du chud → “grasps” (C03:357), while chud pa on its own is banked as “side” and “fit” — so only the negation ma is taken out as a member, carrying “failed”.
