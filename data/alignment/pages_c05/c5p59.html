AI NOTE —

A four-line root verse from the Treasure House of Knowledge, chapter IV, quoted inside Gendun Drup's commentary. The initial capitals on Inbetween, One and It are verse lineation, not apparatus. Geshe Michael's English lines do not track the Tibetan lines: his English opens with the Tibetan's second line (the count) before its first (the beings and the realm), so the first half of the page crosses.

Lines 1–2 are checked against the commentary that unpacks them. C05:177 restates this verse in prose — srid pa bar ma 'dod khams su … 'phen pa rnam pa nyi shu rtsa gnyis → twenty-two different projections had with inbetween beings in the realm of desire — which independently carries srid pa bar ma → Inbetween beings, rnam pa → different, 'phen pa → projections and the locative su → in. The verse writes nyi shu gnyis where the commentary writes nyi shu rtsa gnyis: the connective rtsa is dropped to hold the seven-syllable line, not misspelt, and all four lines scan at exactly seven.

The two demonstratives are treated differently, and that is deliberate. Line 3's de is left with no English: Geshe Michael renders it One, a numeral pronoun counting off one of the twenty-two, and that is a recast rather than an exponent of the demonstrative — a real numeral, gcig, stands two words later in the same verse. Line 4's de IS given It. The two copulas yin are likewise left unrendered, following this course's convention.

rigs → discrete type is the span to challenge first. The verse abbreviates; C05:178 comments on this very line with the full term, ris mthun gcig kho na yin pa'i phyir → is counted but a single discrete being, which is also what licenses but on kho na and single on gcig here. discrete may belong to that technical frame (ris mthun, nikaya-sabhaga) rather than to rigs itself, whose plain value is type; the campaign already made the same call at C05:166, banking bare rigs → discrete being.

Left unwrapped as English Geshe Michael supplied (there is no Tibetan under it): had with, the frame One is the type with, the article a in but a single, and is twice.
