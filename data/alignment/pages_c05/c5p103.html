AI NOTE — Two reason-clauses (… phyir dang, … phyir) answer an objection. The first is a where-it-was-said reason: the quotation belongs to a section (skabs) examining whether an object and the awareness of it must match in existing or not existing, so it does not bear on the present point. The second is the craving-spirit case: the river seen as pus and blood. Geshe Michael renders only the FIRST phyir as “for”; the closing one has no marker in the English. dbu sems gnyis is the standard abbreviation of dbu ma and sems tsam and is banked WHOLE — dbu on its own does not mean “Middle Way” and sems on its own does not mean “Mind-Only”, so no member is cut from it; gnyis (“the two”) has no exponent, because Geshe Michael writes “the schools of” instead. mi mtshungs (“not equivalent”) likewise has none: the whole equivalent/not-equivalent pair is folded into “the question of whether”. GESHE MICHAEL'S ENGLISH (VERBATIM)
Yet there is no such problem. The point of this quotation is to say that the burning steel and so on are not something that was created by some unchanging creator being or something like that; by someone who thought it over first and then created them.
