AI NOTE — RECONCILED from a Tibetan-first and an English-first proposal. The three real disagreements were settled against the landed bank, not by taste.

THE COMPOUND sgom spang. One analyst split it flat (sgom = “habituation”, spang = “eliminated”, the rest unwrapped); the other took the whole phrase “undesirable object eliminated by the path of habituation” under it. C05:135 settles it: the exactly parallel compound mthong spang is banked there as one d=5 → “eliminated by the path of seeing”, with members mthong → “seeing” and spang → “eliminated”. That shape is followed here exactly — and it stops at “eliminated”: the noun head “undesirable object” is Geshe Michael's supplied head for the nominalised spang bya and stays UNWRAPPED, as “every” comes separately from ma lus.

UNDER-READ, DELIBERATE. gnas gyur is the transformation of the basis, but Geshe Michael's English carries only “state” for it. Rather than bank the dictionary pair whole and let “state” silently absorb gyur, gnas takes “state” and gyur is nulled — the honest reading of an unrendered half. Likewise dri ma med pa = “unstained” is kept flat: only the negation med → “un” is claimed as a member, because dri ma is the noun “stain” and the bank has never glossed it “stained”.

SUPPLIED, NOT ALIGNED. A good deal here has no Tibetan under it and is left unwrapped: the opening “Finally,”; the “or” joining phan and gnod (there is no dang); “also”; “a person” / “the person”; the whole frame “the state where he achieved” in the first sentence — dgra bcom pa'i 'bras bu is just the arhat's fruit, and that first “achieved” is expansion, not thob; the second “has just” (there is no second ma thag pa); and “here is that” around phyir. dgra bcom pa is the arhat, the foe-destroyer, which Geshe Michael unpacks literally as “destroying the enemy”.
