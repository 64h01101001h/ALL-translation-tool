Coveting consists of a wrong (that is, an improper) desire for another's wealth, which leads to a wish that one make it his own. AI NOTE — A root verse line of the Treasure House of Knowledge [iv.311b], quoted bare; the commentary at C05:364 unpacks it word by word and is the witness for most of this page. 'di la → "In them": Geshe Michael renders bcu po 'di la at 364 as "in reference to "them"--to the group of ten just listed", so "them" for the demonstrative is his own rendering at both rows, not an inference, and the locative la carries the "In". las → "deeds" and kyang → "as well" are confirmed at 364 too (lus ngag gi bdun ni las kyang yin → "the seven ... are action or deeds themselves, as well as paths"). The verse-final yin is left UNLINKED: it closes the second line (las kyang yin), while the single "are" in the English stands in the first ("Three are paths"), whose Tibetan copula is elided — the commentary supplies one there (lam yin pa dang). Pairing them would pass every mechanical check and still assert the wrong line, so the "are" is left unwrapped as a supplied copula. The topic particle ni has no exponent.
