The environmental result of ill will (gnod sems), in the frame Geshe Michael uses through 199–206: a bare topic phrase, then a dang-chain of what such a world holds. "Because you have", "you live in a world of", "where there are", "you live surrounded by" are his supplied frame and stay unwrapped.

The one crossing, and how it was settled. The two analysts split on yams and nad 'go ba: one read yams="plague" with nad 'go ba="diseases spread", the other yams="chaos" with nad 'go ba="plague". Neither reading is what the binding dictionary records. Geshe Michael's own glossary (HTG2016) glosses yams as "chaos" and nad 'go ba as "diseases" — and those are the only two English words in this row's vocabulary for them, since nad 'go ba occurs in just one other passage (MB2:429, "some disease has raised its head"). AI NOTE — The vessel-world ripening of wrong views, from the Treasure House of Knowledge chapter IV account of how each of the ten non-virtues degrades the outer world. The Tibetan is a chain of dang-joined results: the supreme and chief sources sink away; things that are unclean and suffering appear as supremely clean and as happiness; and one comes to be without dwelling, without protector, without refuge. Three words in this line were deliberately left unlinked, each because the English word sitting in its slot says something the Tibetan word does not. gtso bo ("chief, principal") — the “single” of "the single highest" is an English intensifier on the superlative, and the bank's 21 existing gtso bo links are all in the principal / chief / main family, none of them "single". mchog tu ("supremely") — Geshe Michael wrote “actually”, a word about truth rather than degree. snang ba ("appear, seem") — he recast "X appear as Y" into "people think that X are Y", so "think" belongs to his new frame; all 12 English-bearing snang ba links in the layer are appear / seem / appearance, and keying a word of appearance to a mental verb would be a false entry. In the closing triad the same test splits the pair: gnas takes "place" and skyob pa ("protector") takes his loose "help", but skyabs ("refuge, shelter") is left null, because the English standing over it is "nothing to protect you" and "protect" is the meaning of skyob, the word immediately before it in this very sentence. gtsang ba appears twice and is linked twice — as "clean" inside "unclean", and as “nice”, which is Geshe Michael's loose rendering of "pure" in this line and not a general equivalent. "of happiness", "from the earth" and "people think" have no Tibetan under them and are left unwrapped. Finally, "and happiness" is an outlier: the Tibetan bde bar is a second complement of snang ba parallel to gtsang ba, and two independent witnesses (C09:163 and C17:510, different courses and different page numbers) read "and happy". The text here is quoted as it stands and the divergence is filed as an erratum. The page number and the Reading Five heading after "protect you." are ingest carry-over, not part of the sentence.

AI NOTE — A citation header rather than a sentence of the Abhidharma text. The Tibetan is one ablative noun phrase — from the Illumination of the True Thought composed by the great Lord Tsongkapa — and Geshe Michael has turned it into an English sentence. “The selections are all taken” is his supplied frame: the Tibetan has no noun for selections and no verb of taking, so that whole opening stays unwrapped, and the only exponent in it is las → “from”. The row is the structural twin of C05:27 and C05:146: AGENT + agentive + brtsoms pa'i + TITLE + las.

Two things in the Tibetan carry no English. dbu ma (Middle Way), the qualifier distinguishing Tsongkapa’s commentary on Entering the Middle Way, is dropped from Geshe Michael’s standing short title, so it is banked as a nulled word outside the title rather than swallowed into it — the shape already landed at C05:146, where mdzod Tik sits outside thar lam gsal byed the same way. And chen po (great) is dropped too; because the agentive -s rides on it as chen pos it cannot be spanned bare the way C05:27’s gyis → “by” was, and a d=5 chen pos → “by” would key great to by in the dictionary harvest. The “by” is therefore left unwrapped.
