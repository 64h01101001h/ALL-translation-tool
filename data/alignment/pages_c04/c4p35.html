AI NOTE — A citation from Kedrup Tenpa Dargye's Tik chen, the Ocean of Reasoning (rigs pa'i rgya mtsho), the great commentary on the Commentary on Valid Perception. The Tibetan is one long nominalized clause, gang zhig ... de nas ... bsnyad par bya ba yin: "whoever ... from that point on is to be spoken of as ...". GMR rearranges heavily: the relative gang zhig heads the English subject ("Any person who is able to develop"), while the verb skyes pa ("has arisen") closes its own Tibetan clause. GMR's frame words "is able to", "has ... become someone whom we can", "in", "with the" and "says" are left unwrapped. rgyud is claimed only for "stream": "of his mind" is GMR's unpacking of the mind-stream sense, not a separate Tibetan word. The ablative las after sdug bsngal is the one case particle with an exponent ("from"); the earlier las on the title and the locative la after rgyud are nulled. zhes bya ba'i sgras is literally "by the word called ...": only sgras is claimed, against "name".
