AI NOTE — A source caption for a citation from the Jewel of the True Thought. GMR reads it English-first — work, author, monastery — the reverse of the Tibetan, which puts monastery and author first and the title last. The wylie is corrupt at the author's name: it reads ser smad dge bshes dge bshes dbang phyug, with dge bshes keyed twice where the second should be ye shes (C04:63, 79, 181 ser smad dge bshes ye shes dbang phyug); the ACIP column carries the same doubled reading, so the error is in the source of record, not the ingest. The names are transcriptions, not translations, so ser smad ('Sera Mey'), dge bshes ('Geshe') and dbang phyug ('Wangchuk') are banked as nulls per the C04:51 ruling, and 'Yeshe' has nothing under it at all. brtsoms pa'i ('composed by') has no exponent — GMR compresses authorship to the bare 'by' — and the agentive gis rides that same hinge, so both are unrendered. Inside tshad ma dgongs rgyan, dgongs carries 'Thought' and rgyan ('ornament') GMR's 'Jewel', as on C04:38 and 79; 'True' has no exponent and tshad ma is not carried into the short title. 'Tibetan Buddhist Monastery' is GMR's gloss for his readers with nothing in the wylie beneath it.
