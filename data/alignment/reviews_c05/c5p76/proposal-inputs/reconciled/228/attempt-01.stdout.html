The Master, to demonstrate how this way of answering reflects exactly the position of the Arya [Nagarjuna], quotes the following statement from the Root Text on Wisdom: Because the karma never begins, Because it has no nature of its own, And since it has never even begun, Neither then can it simply go away. The lines are saying that, because there is no such thing as a karma that exists through its own nature, no such karma could ever have a beginning that came in and of itself. Because of this, it is completely impossible for a karma ever to end by any nature of its own.
