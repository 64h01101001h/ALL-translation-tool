# C05:230 — Codex reconciliation decisions

Both title parents are retained without the supplied The. The final member takes the English proposal's rigs pa/Reasoning rather than the Tibetan numeral drug cu/Sixty: either is a legitimate unique tighter member, but Reasoning supplies the more useful lexical component. Only one immediately following member is claimed.
English p0 preserves the active/passive causal roles as a complete recast: the world has ignorance as condition, or ignorance causes the world. The final page recovers world and ignorance as d5 atoms. Tibetan rkyen/causes plus can/null is not retained. HGM 487 lists causes but does not establish that this token is a plural noun or causal verb, and can contributes the possession-of-condition relation. The conversion is distributed across the English predicate frame; neither rkyen nor can is asserted absent. rkyen can has no exact HGM entry. Complete p0 is defensible, but useful local coverage is preferred without inventing an isolated causal dictionary equivalent.
English w5 gang phyir/Why? has direct HGM support as a possible translation, but in this particular syntax gang phyir introduces the Buddha-speech reason. The final d3 gang phyir/For retains that causal use. The separately inserted Why? is not banked twice under the same Tibetan. rdzogs pa'i is omitted: no perfect or fully enlightened exponent appears before Buddhas. sangs rgyas/Buddhas and gsungs/say target the post-Buddhas verb, not the earlier title say.
English p2 de yi phyir na/why then is narrowed to then. The actual cis later in the Tibetan licenses why. English p3 zhes cis mi 'thad/would it be wrong to say incorrectly excludes why while retaining cis; the final page keeps zhes/to say, cis/why and the complete negative mi 'thad/wrong. 'thad alone is not matched to wrong. The hypothetical auxiliaries would it be remain unwrapped. The source 'di owns this, 'jig rten the second world, yin the is after this world, and rnam rtog/conceptions is lexical. The English only has no independently selected limiting Tibetan particle in the verse and is unwrapped.
English p4 meaning of this verse is a defensible citation-based phrase, but final don/meaning and 'grel par/commentary preserve lexical words. ces gsungs pa'i is omitted from the word layer; the English of this verse, according to the frame is not forced into separate atoms.
English w11 is accepted whole as final w21. Exact HGM 22903 attests 'jig rten rnams/many worlds. Therefore Tibetan rnams/null was undercoverage in this context: many is available as part of the established plural unit. Its 'jig rten/worlds member is unique and strictly tighter; plural number in the English noun is inherited from the parent, not claimed as a separate rnams gloss.
English w12 is extended to the exact whole HGM 18277 equivalent through some essence of its own. Tibetan rang/gi/ngo bos fragmentation loses the instrumental expression; the final established unit preserves it. rang licenses reflexive ownership, gi the possessive relation, ngo bo essence, final instrumental -s through. some is the English indefinite force internal to the attested unit. The unique ngo bo/essence member is literal, and no separate subword -s is banked. This whole unit is d5 because it is an established HGM lexical expression; it is not justified merely by substring containment.
The Tibetan p26 ma/none and w27 grub pa/does so are retained with exact first-none and first-does-so ranges. English turns the predicate negation into a negative subject quantifier: none of the many worlds ... does so. ma supplies the negative force; its d6 token does not assert a dictionary noun or an extra participant. grub pa supplies the existential predicate resumed by does so (the antecedent verb is exist), so the pro-verb is kept at d3. This is reviewed at the full clause level, not inferred from the English word none alone.
English p5 products of our conception is rejected as a whole correspondence: it has a supplied our and lacks an exponent for the limiting tsam. Final rtog pas/conception and btags pa/products retain clear lexical contributions; products is a nominal contextual rendering of designation/creation, consistent with HGM btags pa creation and the original source relation. The restrictive tsam is distributed over none of them are anything more than and is omitted, not nulled. of our and the repeated negative-subject frame remain unwrapped.
All five omitted Tibetan runs are inventoried in unwrapped-ranges.json. las, rkyen can du, rdzogs pa'i, ces gsungs pa'i, and tsam du bshad cing are citation grammar, an unrendered modifier, or distributed recast content as described above; none is falsely asserted to have no meaning.
All repetitions were checked explicitly: w4/world at English 47 is first verse world; w12/world at 172 is second; w21/many worlds at 275 is commentary plural. w10/say is English 110, after Buddhas, not title say at 30. p15/is is English 178, not earlier is. ma/none is first English none at 263, not the later repeated none at 339. The essence relation spans 306:337, avoiding the Tibetan proposal's superseded wrong meaning-of occurrence. conception at 391 belongs to rtog pas, distinct from verse conceptions at 186.
The punctuation candidate is refuted under the required default. The interrogative sense is clear, but the change from period to question mark is not independently established as an editorial defect; retained verse punctuation is defensible as literary convention. Full original witnesses, exact query strings/counts, registered-class checks and all five refutation grounds are saved in errata-refutation.json and errata-evidence-original.json.
