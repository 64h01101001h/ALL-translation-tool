GESHE MICHAEL'S ENGLISH (VERBATIM)
The Sixty Verses on Reasoning say as well, The world is something that ignorance causes; Why? For the Buddhas say it is so. And why then would it be wrong to say That this world is only conceptions. The meaning of this verse, according to the commentary, is that none of the many worlds that exist does so through some essence of its own: none of them are anything more than products of our conception.
