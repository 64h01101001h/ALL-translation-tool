# C05:229 — Codex reconciliation decisions

The independent English proposal's p1 is rejected: 'di la gnyis does not license the bracketed still another one, and section/has/parts framing is not separately banked. Tibetan w1/w2 retain This/two.
English p2 and p3 are decomposed into the Tibetan proposal's secure lexical atoms. rtog pa'i/dbang/gis owns conceptualization/process/through; 'jog owns established and tshul how. bden 'dzin is the attested whole grasp to true existence with its unique 'dzin/grasp member. de las and the extended existing-in-the-opposite-way construction are distributed; bzlog/opposite and 'dzin pa'i/holds remain locally secure. bstan pa'o is assigned once to describe, not also to the earlier supplied First we will show.
English w1 and w2 lose initial the/The. The source dang po licenses first, but no standalone article. The Upali work-title convention licenses Sutra Requested by Upali as the established complete abbreviated title (original ILL:1104 and current C05:229 support this title); the exact glossary entry has no HGM field, so this is corpus title evidence, not a claim of glossary attestation. No Sutra morpheme is invented. nye bar 'khor/Upali is the literal unique member.
sna tshogs/splash has HGM 10994 support; yid dga'/pleasing is a contextual adjective supported by the current verse and original ILL:1104. me tog/flowers is lexical. English w6 is accepted whole: HGM 1063 gives open their petals. kha bye is the flower-opening idiom; the body part is rendered as petals in a flower context, with the possessive relating it back to those flowers. This is one attested idiom, not a separate claim that kha literally means their or petals in all contexts. Tibetan w22/open was unnecessarily narrow.
English p4 is defensible as a complete poetic line, but final alignment preserves Golden/palaces/blaze as useful independently supported lexical words. The two ornamental modifiers are deliberately omitted. HGM 4925 for mchog includes breathtaking beauty; HGM 25091 for yid 'ong ba gives pleasant. Local mchog modifies khang, whereas yid 'ong ba qualifies its pleasing appearance. ILL:1104 explicitly renders Perfect palaces and lovely light. Thus Tibetan w26 assigning the entire breathtaking beauty to yid 'ong ba cannot be securely isolated. The glossary's mchog equivalent is weighed but not used to assert that yid 'ong ba is absent. No null is created for either modifier.
English p5 is rejected as a complete mapping because the source has an existential denial of a maker while GMR adds the search imperative and its you/him apparatus. byed pa/maker remains secure. ILL:1104 has byed pa while C14:129/C18:871 have byed po; this variation establishes no spelling defect in the exact C05 row.
English w7 all of these is narrowed to these. de dag has HGM these/all these, but dag alone is not separately asserted to be a universal quantifier in this occurrence. English all of is left unwrapped. English p6 and p7 are replaced by secure rtog pa'i/conceptions, bzhag pa/built, 'jig rten/world, and rnam brtags/invention. The two verse dbang gis expressions are compressed into the respective of relations; no independent process word exists in either verse clause, and no null is assigned to distributed relational content.
English p8 is decomposed: the retrospective gsungs supplies describing and the following lexical construction supplies objects/established/through/process/conceptualization. The verse is an anaphoric English subject; it has no separately selected Tibetan noun. It is left unwrapped.
English w8 and m8 are accepted with final ids w41/m42. HGM 4747 directly attests chos thams cad/every single object in the universe. This is an established whole unit for all existing phenomena; in the universe expresses its unrestricted domain, not a separately invented Tibetan place name. chos/object is unique and strictly tighter in both parent ranges. Tibetan thams cad/every single plus chos/object is replaced by this useful attested whole.
English p9 is decomposed into rtog pas/conceptions, btags pa/creation and tsam/nothing more than. HGM 6498 and 14946 support the creation and restrictive equivalents. The article a and genitive of are left unwrapped; no second independent agent or participant is claimed.
English p10 is rejected despite mechanical success. Its source contains rtog pa'i dbang gis bzhag par, but the claimed English there are as well many other statements of the Buddha that describe how does not contain that conceptual-establishment predicate. Depth 3 cannot hide this distributed correspondence. The preceding Tibetan rtog pas/btags pa/tsam anchors the English creation expression; the final repeated conceptual-establishment phrase is omitted as distributed repetition, not absent meaning. gsungs pa/statements, gzhan/other, yang/as well and mang/many retain their actual reordered English occurrences. of the Buddha and that describe how remain unwrapped; no separately expressed Buddha noun is present.
All omitted case/citation/copula/connective forms are inventoried in unwrapped-ranges.json. la (outline), ste, ni, las, zhing, gyi, yin, te, zhes and ngo do not receive speculative dictionary English. de las and 'di na de la'ang participate in larger recasts; 'ga' med de in the maker-search denial is not equivalent to find. The two rnams nulls alone assert proven plural fusion into objects at English 88:95 and 603:610; they do not label uncertainty.
Repeated source occurrences were manually checked against the canonical resolver: all five rtog-pa-family selections have distinct intended ranges. The last rtog pa'i at 478 is deliberately omitted; it is not attached to the English conceptions at 816. The two process/through/object/established sets remain in their respective outline and explanation sentences. Every final range is listed below and in resolved-spans.json.
