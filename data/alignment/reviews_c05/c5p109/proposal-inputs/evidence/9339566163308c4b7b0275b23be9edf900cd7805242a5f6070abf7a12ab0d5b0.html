GESHE MICHAEL'S ENGLISH (VERBATIM)
According to the claim of certain other groups, deeds which bring one an experience in the hells are to be understood as the "black" ones. And those that bring one an experience in some other birth in the desire realm are to be understood as the two: as deeds that are both white and black. Still other groups assert that those kinds of deeds which the path of seeing eliminates are the black ones, while the other deeds that come from the desire are both white and black.
