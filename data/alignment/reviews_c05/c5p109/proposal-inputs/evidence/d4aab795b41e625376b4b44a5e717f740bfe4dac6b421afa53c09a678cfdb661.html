Segment 327 — Roots of undertaking and the virtuous paths

TIBETAN (WYLIE)
sbyor ba rtsa ba gsum las skyes, ,de yi mjug thogs las byung phyir, ,brnab sems sogs rtsa gsum las skyes, ,dge ba sbyor dang mjug bcas rnams, ,chags sdang gti mug med las skyes
GESHE MICHAEL'S ENGLISH (VERBATIM)
The undertakings come from the root three. Because they occur just subsequent to them, Coveting and the rest come from these three. The virtues, with undertaking and conclusion, From no desire, dislike, ignorance.
AI NOTE — PROVISIONAL — independently proposed by Codex, English-first. The single med licenses the shared no over desire, dislike and ignorance; the verse is not read as making dislike or ignorance virtuous. The commentary at C05:333 makes this scope explicit. de claims them, not supplied they. rtsa in 'these three' and final skyes have no separate English token here and are omitted. brnab sems and gti mug stay whole because their single-word translations offer no meaningful tighter member.
