Retain g-yog 'khor → people who work at d3 as the servant-group recast, supported by C10:397–398 circle of servants. - w31: Replace the atomic ma nges pa → lack plus spobs → confidence with d3 ma nges pa'i spobs → lack of confidence. The source combines indeterminacy/uncertainty with confidence; the negative confidence recast is defensible whole. Verified glossary lack for ma nges pa corroborates the recast, but does not justify a general dictionary equation in isolation. The possessive case links the constituents; no supplied participant is captured.

- w35: Omit mi phan pa → without help despite verified glossary without help. Here mi phan pa is the object of tshol, seeking what does not help; English makes the person find themself without help. The inverse help-giving/receiving structure is not settled by glossary overlap. Do not null.

- w36: Omit first tshol ba → finding with the disputed first help recast. Find is a verified HGM equivalent elsewhere, but finding yourself is a reflexive result idiom here and does not securely map this search predicate.

- w37: Omit phan pa → help. Attempt 1 intended the second positive noun, but independent actual-range inspection showed the forward cursor selected phan pa inside the first mi phan pa and English help inside without help. Retaining that would smuggle the unresolved first recast back in. This semantically rejected attempt is preserved; final attempt 2 removes the span. No uncertainty null is used to advance the cursor.

- w38: Omit mi → never with second help predicate: negative search and negative attainment are different claims; isolated negation does not settle the predicate recast.

- w39: Omit second tshol ba → find with the unresolved help-agency construction. A glossary find does not independently prove the source's help-direction or attained-result force here.

- w40: Retain gzhan → others in active hurting others; source la marks target. English atom contains only the target noun.

- w41: Retain gnod pa byed pa → hurting at d3: harm plus do form the causative action predicate. Others is separately mapped to source gzhan la.

- w42: Retain gzhan → others in passive being hurt by others; source gyis marks agent. The English target is the last others, not the active target or earlier talk-to-you expansion.

- w43: Retain gnod pa byed par → hurt at d3, resolved after hurting. Source has an active predicate with gzhan gyis agent, rendered English passive with the same external agent. Do not claim Tibetan itself is morphologically passive; being and by are left unwrapped.
