GESHE MICHAEL'S ENGLISH (VERBATIM)
The three of "being thrown out, and reaching, and attaining," are, lastly, Being thrown out from the ranks of men. Reaching the ranks of dogs. Attaining the ranks of the gods.gsum (final)→folded
"Aiming your mind ultimately to practice" means to practice religion with the following thoughts: This time I've been able to obtain a good human body and circumstances; they are extremely hard to find, they are incredibly valuable, and they include all the necessary leisure and fortune. This is the one and only time I will have such a life. And it will not be here long; it is absolutely sure that I will die, I have no way of knowing when my death will come. And when I die, only this holy spiritual practice will be of any use to me.ma gtogs→within "only" All the things and honors I have gathered in this life, every bit of fame I've gained, everything else of the money and possessions I may have with me, will not be the slightest help to me.ji tsam→folded

Segment 175 — let me become a beggar

TIBETAN (WYLIE)
chos phugs sprang la gtad pa ni, 'o na tshe 'di'i bde thabs ma bsgrubs par chos byas na chos kyi mthun rkyen tsam yang med par sprang po byas mi yong ngam snyam na, ngas chos kyi phyir du dka' ba spyad nas sprang po byas na'ang byas, zas gos ngan ngon slong mo brgyab pa tsam gyis 'tsho bar byas nas chos sgrub nus par byed snyam pa'o,
GESHE MICHAEL'S ENGLISH (VERBATIM)
"Aiming your practice ultimately to the beggar" is like this: Suppose you think to yourself "But well now, if I stop trying to do what it takes to live well in this life so I can do my spiritual practice, I'm afraid that I won't even find the bare necessities for doing the practice: I'll become a beggar."tsam→folded Think then to yourself this way: "I will undertake any hardship for my practice; and if it means I have to become a mere beggar, then let me become a beggar. I will find a way to do my practice, even if I have to live on lousy scraps of food that I beg off others, and wear any old clothes they give me."tsam→folded
AI NOTE — (1) THE TWO-VOICE MEDITATION: the fear-thought and the resolve-thought both DRAMATIZED as quotations, with "Think then to yourself this way:" supplied as the pivot — the fullest inner-speech staging in the file. (2) THE FIAT FORMULA: byas na'ang byas ("if done, done") → "then LET me become a beggar" — the concessive doubling rendered as fiat; "mere" supplied. (3) zas gos ngan ngon DISTRIBUTED: "lousy scraps of food that I beg off others" / "any old clothes they give me" — the food/clothes pair split into two clauses with the beggar-scene supplied. (4) mthun rkyen → "the bare necessities" (spread vs seg 139's "things which are conducive to its success"); 'o na → "But well now" (the oral file); mi yong ngam → "I'm afraid that" — the worry-interrogative made an explicit fear.
