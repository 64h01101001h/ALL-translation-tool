Segment 57 — nirvana with something left over

TIBETAN (WYLIE)
nyon sgrib ma lus par spangs shing , snga ma'i las nyon gyi 'bras bur gyur pa'i sdug bsngal gyi phung po dang bcas pa'i so sor brtag 'gog, lhag bcas myang 'das kyi mtshan nyid, mtshan gzhi, nyer len gyi phung po ma dor ba'i nyan thos dgra bcom pa'i rgyud kyi myang 'das lta bu yin
GESHE MICHAEL'S ENGLISH (VERBATIM)
The definition of nirvana with something left over is: "A cessation which comes from the individual analysis, and which consists of having eliminated the mental-affliction obstacles in their entirety, but where one still has the suffering heaps that are a result of his past actions and bad thoughts." A classical example of this would be the nirvana found in the mental stream of a listener who is a foe destroyer, and who has not yet shucked off the heaps he took on.lta bu→"would be"

Segments 58–59 — nothing left over; the nirvana which does not stay

TIBETAN (WYLIE)
nyon sgrib ma lus par spangs shing , snga ma'i las nyon gyi 'bras bur gyur pa'i sdug bsngal gyi phung po dang bral ba'i so sor brtags 'gog, lhag med myang 'das kyi mtshan nyid, mtshan gzhi, nyer len gyi phung po dor ba'i nyan thos dgra bcom pa'i rgyud kyi myang 'das lta bu yin
sgrib gnyis ma lus par spangs pa'i so sor brtag 'gog, mi gnas pa'i myang 'das kyi mtshan nyid, mtshan gzhi, sangs rgyas 'phags pa'i rgyud kyi 'gog bden lta bu
GESHE MICHAEL'S ENGLISH (VERBATIM)
The definition of nirvana with nothing left over is: "A cessation which comes from the individual analysis, and which consists of having eliminated the mental-affliction obstacles in their entirety, and where one is free of the suffering heaps that are a result of his past actions and bad thoughts." A classical example of this would be the nirvana found in the mental stream of a listener who is a foe destroyer, and who has shucked off the heaps he took on.lta bu yin
The definition of nirvana which does not stay is: "A cessation which comes from the individual analysis, and which consists of having eliminated both kinds of obstacles in their entirety." A classical example of this would be the truth of cessation in the mental stream of a realized being who is a Buddha.lta bu
