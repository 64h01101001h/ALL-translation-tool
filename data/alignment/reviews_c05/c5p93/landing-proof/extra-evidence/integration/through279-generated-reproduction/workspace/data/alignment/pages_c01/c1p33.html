We have now reached the third and final of the preliminaries that lead into the composition of the text. This one consists of a strong encouragement for the reader to study the work well, and is contained in the next verse of the root text: (2) Listen with a pure mind, fortunate ones Who have no craving for the pleasures of life, And who to make leisure and fortune meaningful strive To turn their minds to the path which pleases the Victors.de dag ("those")→within "fortunate ones"
