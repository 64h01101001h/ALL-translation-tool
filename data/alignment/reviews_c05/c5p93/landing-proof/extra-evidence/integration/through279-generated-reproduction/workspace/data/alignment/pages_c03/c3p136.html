The first blang dor is kept at phrase depth as dge sdig gi blang dor because GMR's couplet "to do the things we should, and not do what we shouldn't" renders the whole phrase — the should/shouldn't is dge sdig (virtue and sin), so the English cannot honestly be hung on blang dor alone.
