Segment 168 — fivefold division of deeds

TIBETAN (WYLIE)

GESHE MICHAEL'S ENGLISH (VERBATIM)
Some people claim that the kinds of deeds are five: they arrive at this figure by dividing indefinite types of deeds into (1) those which will definitely ripen but at an indefinite time, and (2) those where neither ripening itself nor its timing is definite.The explanatory continuation is marked by the colon; no separate English word.Sentence-final assertion particle has no separate English exponent.
