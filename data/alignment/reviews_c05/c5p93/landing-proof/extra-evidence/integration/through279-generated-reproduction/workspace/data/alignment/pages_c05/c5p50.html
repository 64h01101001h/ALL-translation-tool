"Just how," one may ask, "do you describe virtuous, non-virtuous, and ethically neutral deeds?" Deeds that lead to a pleasant experience are virtuous deeds. Deeds that lead to some "other" type of experience--that is, which bring on a neutral experience--are themselves the "other" type of deeds: those which are ethically neutral.
