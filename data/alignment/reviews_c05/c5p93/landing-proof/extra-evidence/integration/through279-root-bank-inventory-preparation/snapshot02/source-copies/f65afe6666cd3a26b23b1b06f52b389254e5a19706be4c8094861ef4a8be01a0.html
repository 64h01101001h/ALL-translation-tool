Segment 286 — the infinite regression

TIBETAN (WYLIE)
rang gi de ring gi rig pa 'di kha sang gi rig pa'i rig rgyun dang , da lo'i rig pa 'di lo snga ma'i rig pa'i rig rgyun yin pa bzhin du, rang gi tshe 'di'i rig pa 'di tshe snga ma'i rig pa'i rig rgyun dang , tshe snga ma'i rig pa de yang de'i tshe snga ma snga ma'i rig rgyun yin pas de ga nang bzhin phar ded na 'di phan chad du med bya ba gtan nas med par rig pa'i ya mtha' thug med du 'grub cing
GESHE MICHAEL'S ENGLISH (VERBATIM)
Your awareness of today is a mental continuation of the awareness you had yesterday. This year's awareness is a mental continuation of the awareness you had the year before. Just so, your awareness over this entire life is a mental continuation of the awareness you had in your former life. The awareness you had in your former life was, in turn, a mental continuation of the awareness you had in the life before that. You can continue back in a regression like this and absolutely never reach some point where you can say, "Prior to this, I had no awareness." This then proves the infinite regression of one's awareness.rig pa (#3)→within "your awareness"
AI NOTE — (1) THE REGRESSION LADDER: four rungs (today/yesterday, this year/last, this life/former, former/before that), with rig rgyun leveled to "a mental continuation" ×4 — formula-leveling as proof-structure. (2) THE QUOTED HYPOTHETICAL SUPPLIED: 'di phan chad du med → direct speech ("Prior to this, I had no awareness.") — the negated claim staged as a quotation. (3) phar ded spread: "follow this line of thinking to its end" (208) / "continue back in a regression like this" (286) — the chase-idiom as regression. (4) gtan nas med par → "absolutely never". (5) snga ma snga ma (doubled) → "the life before that". (6) 'grub → "This then proves".
