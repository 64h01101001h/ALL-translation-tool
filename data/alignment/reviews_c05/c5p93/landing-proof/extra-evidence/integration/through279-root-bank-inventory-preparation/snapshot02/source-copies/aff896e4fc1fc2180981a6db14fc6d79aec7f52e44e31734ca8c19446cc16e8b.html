AI NOTE — (1) bzhugs so — the honorific "resides/is present" + final so — is the classical bookplate formula, and GMR's "Herein contained is" fronts it exactly as English titles demand; the final particle's ∅ is his period. (2) mdzad pa'i is the HONORIFIC verb "composed" (zhe sa) — "written by" carries the deed, drops the register; the honorific-register pane would flag it, the machine only records it. (3) rje btsun (layer 7: rje "lord" + btsun "reverend") has ∅ exponent — the title vanishes into "Tsongkapa the Great". (4) Two agentives in one title: s on chen pos ("by the Great") and the genitive chain chos kyi rgyal po "King of the Dharma" — both link cleanly.
