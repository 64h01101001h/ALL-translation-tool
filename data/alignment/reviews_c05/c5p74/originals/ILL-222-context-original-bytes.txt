DNGOS PO RANG BZHIN GYIS GRUB PA MED PAR ‘DOD PA’I PHYOGS LA, KUN GZHI SOGS KHAS MA BLANGS KYANG LAS ‘BRAS KYI ‘BREL PA ‘THAD TSUL JI LTAR YIN ZHE NA,

One may begin with the following question:

Just how is it—in the case of those who assert that there is nothing at all which exists through any nature of its own—that we can we can say that the connection between karma and its consequences is all perfectly acceptable, even without accepting concepts such as foundation consciousness?


[1872]
LAS DANG DE’I ‘BRAS BU’I BAR DU YUN RING POR CHOD PA’I DGE MI DGE’I LAS LAS KYANG, BDE BA DANG SDUG BSNGAL SOGS KYI ‘BRAS BU ‘BYUNG BA NI RANG GI SDE PA GONG ‘OG THAMS CAD ‘DOD LA, DE [f. 140b] LA LAS DE ‘BRAS BU ‘BYIN PA’I SNGA LOGS BAR DU GNAS NA NI RTAG PAR ‘GYUR LA, RTAG PAS DON BYED PAR MI NUS PAS LAS LAS ‘BRAS BU ‘BYUNG BA’I ‘BREL PA MI ‘THAD DO,,

The fact is that something is going on between an action and the consequences of that action; which is to say—according to all Buddhist schools, be they higher or lower ones—a person can commit a good or bad deed, and then the result of pleasure or pain and the like can come back to them, even in a case where an extended period of time has passed between this karma and its consequence.  But if, in such a circumstance, the karma somehow remains somewhere during the interim—up to the point at which it gives forth its consequence—then that karma would have to be an unchanging entity.  We all know though that an unchanging entity is incapable of performing a function; as such, the supposed connection between karma and its consequences is an untenable concept.


[1873]
LAS DE BYAS PA’I SKAD CIG GNYIS PAR ZHIG NA NI, MTSAMS DE NAS ‘BRAS BU DNGOS SU ‘BYIN PA’I SNGA LOGS KYI BAR DU LAS DE MED LA, LAS ZHIG PA YANG DNGOS POR MED PAS LAS LAS ‘BRAS BU JI LTAR ‘BYUNG BAR ‘GYUR ZHES BRTZAD PA’I

Someone might then come and make the following argument:

And suppose, rather, that this karma—in the second instant of committing it—comes to a stop.  From this juncture, up to the moment before the karma gives forth its consequence, the karma would not have existed.  And a karma which has already stopped could never exist as a functioning thing; how then could the karma give forth its result?


[1874]
LAN DU, LAS BYAS PA’I SKAD CIG GNYIS PAR ‘GAGS ZIN PA’I SNGA LOGS KYI LAS ‘GAG PA LA MNGON DU PHYOGS PA’I TSE, LAS KYI NUS PA GZHAG PAR BYA BA’I PHYIR KHA CIG KUN GZHI RNAM PAR SHES PA RTOG PAR BYED LA, KHA CIG BU LON GYI DBANG RGYA STE DE’I YI GE DANG ‘DRA BA’I LAS GNYIS LAS DON GZHAN PA, CHUD MI ZA BA ZHES PA LDAN MIN ‘DU BYED DU GYUR PA CIG ‘DOD DO,,

In response to this, some people point to the karma which is approaching stopping: that karma just previous to the point at which the karma has stopped, in the second instant of the commission of the karma.  In this moment, they say, there must be a place where the potential of the karma is deposited; and so it is that some people imagine that there must be a type of awareness known as “foundation consciousness.”  And there are also some who say that there is some other entity, distinct from the two karmas, similar to a letter of credit or receipt for a loan; something—a separate changing entity that is neither physical nor mental—which consists of the fact that the karma will never just fade away.


[1875]
KHA CIG NI LAS GNYIS KYI THOB PA ZHES PA DE GNYIS LAS DON GZHAN DU GYUR PA’I LDAN MIN ZHIG RTOG PAR BYED DO,,

Some other people talk about the “retention for the two karmas,” which they imagine to consist of a changing entity which is neither physical nor mental, and which enjoys an existence of its own, apart from the two.


[1876]
KHA CIG NI LAS KYI BAG CHAGS KYIS BSGOS PA’I RNAM SHES KYI RGYUN RTOG PAR BYED LA, DE’I PHYIR LAS ‘GAGS KYANG LAS KYIS PHYIS YUN RING PO NAS ‘BRAS BU ‘BYIN PA MI ‘GAL PAR ‘DOD DE, LAS KYIS KUN GZHI LA BAG CHAGS ‘JOG PAS BAG CHAGS DE LAS KYI ‘BRAS BU YIN LA, DE’I RIGS ‘DRA BRGYUD PAS MTHAR ‘BRAS BU ‘BYIN PAS DANG PO’I LAS KYI ‘BRAS BU BRGYUD PA LAS ‘BYUNG BAR ‘DOD PA’O,,

Others have an idea about a stream of awareness which is imbued by the deep seed of the karma; as such, they say, there is no contradiction incurred by when we say that a karma gives forth a consequence much, much later, even though the karma itself has stopped.  That is, the karma deposits a deep seed in the foundation consciousness; and thus the consequence of the karmic action is that very deep seed.  The stream of similar cases of the gives forth, finally, a result.  And thus, they believe, the consequence of the original karma is produced from the continuation.
