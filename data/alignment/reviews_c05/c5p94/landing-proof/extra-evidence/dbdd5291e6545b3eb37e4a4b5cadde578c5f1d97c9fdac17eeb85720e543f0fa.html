Segment 46 — why take refuge at all: the five purposes

TIBETAN (WYLIE)
dkon mchog gsum la skyabs su song ba la dgos pa yod de, gnas skabs bsrung ba'i mchog tu 'gyur ba dang , mthar thug sangs rgyas kyi go 'phang thob pa dang , sdom pa thams cad kyi rten byed pa dang , nang pa sangs rgyas par tshud pa dang , ngan 'gro'i skye sgo bcod pa sogs kyi dgos pa yod pa'i phyir,
GESHE MICHAEL'S ENGLISH (VERBATIM)
There is a specific purpose for taking refuge in the Three Jewels.∅→period A temporal purpose is that they can provide you the highest form of protection. The ultimate purpose is to attain the state of enlightenment. Taking refuge also serves as the foundation for all the different kinds of vows. When you take refuge, you thereby join the ranks of the "ones inside": you become a Buddhist. This taking refuge acts as well to slam shut the door to the births of misery. These and others are the purpose for taking refuge in the Three.
