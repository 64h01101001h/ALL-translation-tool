That is a stronger and more useful result than confirmation would have been: a rhythm would suggest deliberate design, whereas drift means a translator cannot infer anything from the person GMR happens to use, and the register surface should say so. Recorded here as disconfirmed rather than left standing in the previous batch's note. ya so'i drung du → "behind your upper teeth"; the palate is SUPPLIED — the Tibetan says only "close to the upper teeth".

GESHE MICHAEL'S ENGLISH (VERBATIM)
[8] As for the breath, see that it makes no noise as you breathe it in. Don't let it be too rough, and don't just remain thoughtless about it.
