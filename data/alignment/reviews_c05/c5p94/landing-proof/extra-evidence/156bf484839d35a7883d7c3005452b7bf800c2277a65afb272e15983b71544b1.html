Segment 489 — mothers, plural: two systems interwoven

TIBETAN (WYLIE)
jo bo'i lugs dang zhi lha'i lugs bsres pa'i sems sgom tshul gsungs te, ma rnams zhes pa'i rnams kyi sgras sems snyoms pa dang , ma zhes pas mar shes tshig snga ma'i snying rje dang
GESHE MICHAEL'S ENGLISH (VERBATIM)
In the verses where he explains how to meditate on the wish for enlightenment too he manages to interweave both systems--the one taught by Lord Atisha and the one which Master Shantideva gave. The fact that the word "mothers" in these verses is plural is meant to refer to the one system, where you exchange your self-concern with a concern for others--a kind of even- mindedness.206 The very mention of the word "mother" at all is meant to refer to the step in the other system where you recognize that all beings are your own mother. And the words that come before the mention of "mothers" are meant to bring out the step of compassion.dang ("and")→clause connective, absorbeddang (trailing "and")→the note continues into 490

Segment 490 — the barley and the field

TIBETAN (WYLIE)
ngang tshul sngar phan btags pa'i ngang tshul mar shes kyi drin bsam lugs dang , slar phan 'dogs pa mnyam pa rje drin dran lugs gnyis kyi phyi ma 'phrul che ste, zhing bzang po la nas btab ma thub kyi sems khral ma gtogs 'di la mang po btab ces drin du brtse ba med pa ltar sems can gyi don bsgrub kyang yus mi 'byung ba yin
GESHE MICHAEL'S ENGLISH (VERBATIM)
When you "think of what's happening"207 here you can do it two ways. You can think of what other beings did for you in the past when they were your mothers; this is how you remember their kindness in the step that follows recognizing them as mothers in the first place, in the one system. On top of that you can also think of what other beings are doing for you even now, every day; this is how it's done in the system where you exchange yourself and others. This second way of doing it gives fantastic results: you no longer have to worry that you lost your chance to plant this fertile field by repaying their kindness then, for here they are around you, lots of fields to plant. It's only your own failure to recognize their present kindness; they are your ticket to fulfilling the needs of every living being, but you don't give them any credit for it.dang ("and")→"On top of that" is supplied; the particle itself absorbedngang tshul (2nd) — "the way [of past benefit]": absorbed into "you can do it two ways"drin dran ("kindness-remembering")→absorbed into "this is how it's done"nas = BARLEY — the seed grain is dropped; English plants the field genericallyces (quote-frame)→the imagined complaint dissolved into narration
