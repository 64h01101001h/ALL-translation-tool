Segment 382 — the four-term grid

TIBETAN (WYLIE)
zhib tu byas na snang tshul la ma dpyad par zhen tshul la dpyad dgos pas dgag bya yang snang yul mi bkag par, zhen yul bkag dgos, 'di dag la rtsa tshig lta bu
GESHE MICHAEL'S ENGLISH (VERBATIM)
If we really get into fine detail, we must analyze not the way that objects appear to us, but rather how we grasp them. Thus it is too with the object we want to see does not exist: it is not that we will deny what appears to us, but rather what we grasp.
