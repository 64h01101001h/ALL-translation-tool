Segment 552 — winds into the central channel

TIBETAN (WYLIE)

GESHE MICHAEL'S ENGLISH (VERBATIM)

As a result a primal state of mind arises, the clear light, the wisdom which is simultaneous. And the power of this wisdom makes all of existence appear as the play of bliss and emptiness.terminative; unrenderedgenitive; unrendered'having done'; no separate exponentgenitive; unrenderedgenitive; unrendered'yoga', head noun of the whole nominalized phrase; no separate exponent
