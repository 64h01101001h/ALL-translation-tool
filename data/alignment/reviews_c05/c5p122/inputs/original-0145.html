GESHE MICHAEL'S ENGLISH (VERBATIM)
One may ask why the expression "path of action" is used in reference to "them"--to the group of ten just listed. The name derives first of all from the fact that the three involved with thought are paths by which the "action" or deed of mental movement is expressed. Moreover, the seven involved with body and speech are action or deeds themselves, as well as paths through which mental movement is expressed. Thus they are all "paths of action." The fourth major part in our presentation of the "paths of action" concerns the ways in which one loses, and then regains, his most basic virtue. *********
