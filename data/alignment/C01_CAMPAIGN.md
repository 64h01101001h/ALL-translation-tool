# The Pabongka campaign — C01 commentary at seven layers

The *Key that Unlocks the Door to the Noble Path* (Pabongka's
commentary on the Three Principal Paths), corpus C01:40–496, ~457
segments — roughly ten C02-readings of material. Worked in passage
batches; each batch adds pages under `pages_c01/` (prefix `c`),
extends the COURSES table in `tools/build_alignment_layer.py`, and
re-runs the builder so the pairs reach the evidence cards. The
letter-exact battery gates every batch.
