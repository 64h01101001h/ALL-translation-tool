Segment 31 — the Buddha Jewel defined and divided

TIBETAN (WYLIE)
don gnyis mthar phyin pa'i skyabs gnas mthar thug, sangs rgyas dkon mchog gi mtshan nyid, de la kun rdzob pa'i sangs rgyas dkon mchog dang , don dam pa'i sangs rgyas dkon mchog gnyis yod
GESHE MICHAEL'S ENGLISH (VERBATIM)
The definition of the Buddha Jewel is "That ultimate place of refuge, the one which has completely satisfied both the needs." There are two kinds of Buddha Jewel: the apparent Buddha Jewel, and the ultimate Buddha Jewel.
DEFINITION FORMULA Tibetan: [definiens], [definiendum]-gi mtshan nyid — the definition comes FIRST
├─ DEFINIENS don gnyis mthar phyin pa'i skyabs gnas mthar thug
│  ├─ don gnyis "the two aims" (one's own and others') "both the needs"
│  ├─ mthar phyin pa'i "gone to the end" + pa'i "the one which has completely satisfied"
│  └─ skyabs gnas mthar thug "That ultimate place of refuge" — mthar thug postposed in Tibetan, fronted in English
├─ DEFINIENDUM sangs rgyas dkon mchog gi mtshan nyid "The definition of the Buddha Jewel is" — GMR inverts the formula; clause links cross
└─ DIVISION de la…gnyis yod "There are two kinds of…" — de la "to that" becomes the English "of" phrase; dang "and"

Segment 32 — the eight qualities, and the Higher Line verse

TIBETAN (WYLIE)
sangs rgyas dkon mchog de 'dus ma byas sogs yon tan brgyad dang ldan te, rgyud bla ma las, ,'dus ma byas shing lhun gyis grub, ,gzhan gyi rkyen gyis rtogs min pa, ,mkhyen dang brtse dang nus par ldan, ,don gnyis ldan pa'i sangs rgyas nyid, ,ces gsungs pa'i phyir
GESHE MICHAEL'S ENGLISH (VERBATIM)
This Buddha Jewel posseses eight different fine qualities, beginning with the quality of being uncaused. As the Higher Line states, This is the One, the Buddha: He is uncaused, He is spontaneous, He is realized by no other way; He has knowledge, and love, and power; He has satisfied both the needs.
VERSE (rgyud bla ma / Uttaratantra I.5-class list) four lines, each a quality cluster; shing coordinates inside line one
├─ 'dus ma byas neg. infix ma: "not compounded" "He is uncaused"
├─ lhun gyis grub gyis agentive used adverbially: "by spontaneity" "He is spontaneous"
├─ gzhan gyi rkyen gyis rtogs min pa gen.+agentive stack: "not realized through others' conditions" "He is realized by no other way"
├─ mkhyen dang brtse dang nus par ldan par terminative on the last conjunct "He has knowledge, and love, and power"
└─ don gnyis ldan pa'i sangs rgyas nyid nyid emphatic: "the very Buddha" "This is the One, the Buddha… He has satisfied both the needs"

Segments 33–34 — the Dharma Jewel and the Sangha Jewel

TIBETAN (WYLIE)
'gog lam gang rung gis bsdus pa'i rnam byang bden pa chos dkon mchog gi mtshan nyid, , de la sgras brjod rigs kyi sgo nas dbye na, , don dam pa'i chos dkon mchog dang , kun rdzob pa'i chos dkon mchog gnyis yod
rig grol gyi yon tan brgyad po gang rung dang ldan pa'i 'phags pa dge 'dun dkon mchog gi mtshan nyid, de la sgras brjod rigs kyi sgo nas dbye na, kun rdzob pa'i dge 'dun dkon mchog dang , don dam pa'i dge 'dun dkon mchog gnyis yod
GESHE MICHAEL'S ENGLISH (VERBATIM)
The definition of the Dharma Jewel is "The enlightened side of truth, either in the form of a cessation, or in the form of a path, or both." In name only this Jewel can be divided into two kinds: the ultimate Dharma Jewel, and the apparent Dharma Jewel.
The definition of the Sangha Jewel is "A realized being who possesses any number of the eight fine qualities of knowledge and liberation." In name only, this Jewel can be divided into two kinds: the ultimate Sangha Jewel, and the apparent Sangha Jewel.
SHARED FORMULAE this folio runs on two recurring frames, both now attested three times
├─ DEFINITION [definiens] X-gi mtshan nyid → "The definition of X is '…'" (inverted every time)
└─ DIVISION de la sgras brjod rigs kyi sgo nas dbye na → "In name only, this Jewel can be divided"
   ├─ sgras brjod rigs "the class of verbal expression" "In name only" — GMR's compression of the scholastic tag
   ├─ sgo nas "through the door of" ∅ · na conditional "can be"
   └─ 'gog lam gang rung gis bsdus pa'i "subsumed by cessation or path, whichever" "either in the form of a cessation, or in the form of a path, or both"
