Segment 49 — the sutra lines, and the span of the root text

TIBETAN (WYLIE)
dang po la, mdo las, shA ri'i bu 'di la chos thams cad rnam pa thams cad du mngon pa rdzogs par 'tshang rgya bar 'dod pas shes rab kyi pha rol tu phyin pa la bslab par bya'o, , de dang de 'dod pas sher phyin la bslab par bya, zhes pa'i mdo'i sbas don theg chen sems bskyed kyi ngo bo dang bcas pa 'chad par byed pa la, sems bskyed pa ni zhes pa nas, rtsa gnyis so, , zhes pa'i bar gyi rtsa 'grel rnams gsungs
GESHE MICHAEL'S ENGLISH (VERBATIM)
Here is the first. We find the following lines in sutra: Sharibu, those who wish to gain total enlightenment, a knowledge of every kind of thing, must train themselves in the perfection of wisdom. Those who wish this, and that, must train themselves in the wisdom perfection. The root text and commentary include other lines that begin with "The wish for enlightenment is" and continue up to "the twenty-two." The function of these latter sections is to clarify the hidden meaning of the words of the sutra, including as it does the essential nature of the wish for enlightenment.
AI NOTE — the layer-7 showpiece: the sutra states the full term shes rab kyi pha rol tu phyin pa and then its contraction sher phyin (shes rab→sher + phyin) — and GMR's English varies in the same two slots: "the perfection of wisdom" for the full form, "the wisdom perfection" for the contraction. The translation preserves the source's own full-vs-short alternation — visible at depth 7, and exactly the kind of pattern the contraction register (bsdus tshig) exists to catch. Also: (1) shA ri'i bu → "Sharibu", his phonetic address form; the capital A is the ACIP Sanskrit-length marker, not an error. (2) bya'o = future-obligative + final 'o → "must" (the imperative force) with the final particle ∅. (3) rtsa gnyis "the twenty-two" = nyi shu rtsa gnyis clipped to its last two syllables — another contraction his quote-boundary "the twenty-two" identifies precisely. (4) nas…bar gyi "from… up to" spans quote-incipit to quote-excipit — his "begin with… and continue up to" is the exact structural exponent.
