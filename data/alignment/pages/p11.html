Segments 53–54 — the colophon and the definition of nirvana

TIBETAN (WYLIE)
, paN chen mkhas grub bstan pa dar rgyas mchog gis brtsams pa'i phar phyin mtha' dpyod nas myang 'das kyi skor,
`, , gnyis pa rang gi lugs la, nyon sgrib ma lus par spangs pa'i so sor brtag 'gog, myang 'das kyi mtshan nyid
GESHE MICHAEL'S ENGLISH (VERBATIM)
From the presentation on Nirvana found in the Analysis of the Perfection of Wisdom, by Kedrup Tenpa Dargye (1493-1568):
Here secondly is the section in which we present our own position. The definition of nirvana is "A cessation which comes from the individual analysis, and which consists of having eliminated the mental-affliction obstacles in their entirety."
AI NOTE — (1) This colophon says mtha' dpyod and the English says "Analysis" — consistent, which sharpens the seg-47 discrepancy: of the four colophons, only 47 mismatches. (2) Layer 7 carries the page's central term: so sor brtag 'gog = so sor "individually" + brtag "analyze" + 'gog "cessation" — the analytical-cessation compound (pratisamkhya-nirodha); GMR's "A cessation which comes from the individual analysis" restores every member as a full word, and the depth-7 hover maps syllable to word one-to-one. (3) myang 'das = myang (mya ngan, "grief") + 'das "passed beyond" — the contraction of mya ngan las 'das pa, "passed beyond grief" → "Nirvana"; seg 61 spells the full form and GMR's verse there says "nirvana beyond grief" — the full/contracted pair mirrored again in his English, as with sher phyin. (4) ma lus par "without remainder" → "in their entirety" — his fixed exponent, attested 4× in Reading Three.

Segments 55–56 — the four nirvanas, and the five synonyms

TIBETAN (WYLIE)
myang 'das la sgras brjod rigs kyi sgo nas dbye na, rang bzhin gyi myang 'das, lhag bcas myang 'das, lhag med myang 'das, mi gnas pa'i myang 'das dang bzhi
rang bzhin gyi myang 'das, rang bzhin gyi yum, rang bzhin gyi sher phyin, rang bzhin gyi chos sku, don dam bden pa rnams don gcig
GESHE MICHAEL'S ENGLISH (VERBATIM)
In name only, nirvana can be divided into the following four types: natural nirvana, nirvana with something left over, nirvana with nothing left over, and nirvana which does not stay.
The following all refer to the same thing: natural nirvana, the natural Mother, the natural perfection of wisdom, the natural Dharma Body, and ultimate truth.
AI NOTE — (1) Layer 7 on the pair that structures the whole topic: lhag bcas = lhag "remainder" + bcas "with" and lhag med = lhag + med "without" — one syllable flips and GMR's exponents flip the same hinge: "with something left over" / "with nothing left over". (2) don gcig "one meaning" → "all refer to the same thing" — the technical equivalence operator of the debate manuals, his exponent sentence-shaped. (3) In seg 56 the corpus English has "and" before "ultimate truth" where the Tibetan list runs bare to rnams — plural particle closing the list, ∅ exponent; his "The following" carries it. (4) sher phyin contracted again — and here his exponent is the FULL "perfection of wisdom", the reverse of seg 49's pairing; the register pane gets both data points. (5) mi gnas pa'i myang 'das: negation mi + "stay" — "nirvana which does not stay", the non-abiding nirvana of a Buddha.
