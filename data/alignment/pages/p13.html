Segment 60 — only wisdom attains it

TIBETAN (WYLIE)
de lta bu'i myang 'das de thabs gang yang rung ba la brten nas thob nus pa ma yin te, bdag med rtogs pa'i shes rab kyi bslab pa de, bslab pa dang po gnyis kyis zin pa'i sgo nas, rtogs zin goms par byas pa la brten nas 'thob pa yin pa'i phyir
GESHE MICHAEL'S ENGLISH (VERBATIM)
The nirvana we are describing here is not something that one can achieve by using any method at all.te→"Rather" Rather, you must achieve it with the training of wisdom, which realizes that nothing has any self nature; this wisdom must be under the influence of the first two trainings, and with it you must habituate yourself to what you were already able to realize.
AI NOTE — (1) The semi-final te after a negated assertion turns adversative, and GMR's "Rather," is its exact exponent — the third distinct English realization of this one particle in the reading (period, colon-for-elaboration, "Rather"), each context-driven; Wilson's account of the semi-final predicts precisely this range. (2) Layer 7: bdag med = bdag "self" + med "not exist" — his "nothing has any self nature" expands two syllables to a clause. (3) The perfective auxiliary zin in rtogs zin ("already realized") surfaces as his "already" — an aspect particle carried by an adverb; and note zin pa two phrases earlier is a DIFFERENT verb ("held by") → "under the influence of" — same syllable, two lexemes, both linked distinctly. (4) The Tibetan attains with thob and closes with 'thob — present and perfect stems of one verb; his "achieve" covers both, stems noted. (5) bslab pa dang po gnyis "the first two trainings" — ethics and concentration, unnamed by both sides.

Segment 61 — the King of Concentration seals it

TIBETAN (WYLIE)
de skad du yang , ting nge 'dzin rgyal po las, ,gal te bdag med chos la so sor rtog, so sor de brtags gang yin sgom byed pa, ,thar pa mya ngan 'das thob rgyu de yin, ,rgyu gzhan dag gis zhi bar 'gyur mi srid, ,ces gsungs pa'i phyir,
GESHE MICHAEL'S ENGLISH (VERBATIM)
This fact is supported by the King of Concentration, which states: Suppose you are able to analyze One by one those things that have no self; And after that you habituate Yourself to what you analyzed individually. This is what then leads you to Achieve your freedom; nirvana beyond grief. It is impossible for any other Cause to bring this peace to you.
