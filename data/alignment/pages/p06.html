Segment 38 — refuge in words and refuge in thoughts

TIBETAN (WYLIE)
de la sgras brjod rigs kyi sgo nas dbye na, rjod byed tshig gi skyabs 'gro dang , ltos pa blo'i skyabs 'gro gnyis yod, dang po ni skyabs 'gro'i tshig lta bu yin
GESHE MICHAEL'S ENGLISH (VERBATIM)
In name only, taking refuge may be divided into two: taking refuge in words, the expression of refuge; and taking refuge in thoughts, the reliance on refuge. An example of the first would be something like the words you use as you take refuge.

Segment 39 — ordinary and exceptional

TIBETAN (WYLIE)
phyi ma la dbye na, skyabs 'gro phal pa dang , khyad par can gnyis yod, skyabs phal pa la rang stobs kyis dpung gnyen du re ba 'cha' ba'i sems pa, dang po'i mtshan nyid, dkon mchog gsum po gang yang rung ba la rang stobs kyis dpung gnyen du re ba 'cha' ba'i sems pa, phyi ma'i mtshan nyid
GESHE MICHAEL'S ENGLISH (VERBATIM)
The latter is of two types: ordinary taking of refuge, and exceptional taking of refuge. The definition of the first is "Any movement of the mind which acts of its own accord, and consists of hoping that some ordinary type of refuge will render one assistance." The definition of the latter is "Any movement of the mind which acts of its own accord, and consists of hoping that any one or number of the Three Jewels will render one assistance."

Layer 7: khyad par can = khyad par "distinction" + possessive can — "having distinction" → "exceptional".

Segment 40 — the five kinds announced

TIBETAN (WYLIE)
de la skyes bu chung ngu dang thun mong ba'i skyabs 'gro, skyes bu 'bring dang thun mong ba'i skyabs 'gro, skyes bu chen po dang thun mong ba'i skyabs 'gro, rgyu'i skyabs 'gro, 'bras bu'i skyabs 'gro dang lnga yod
GESHE MICHAEL'S ENGLISH (VERBATIM)
There are five different kinds of this extraordinary taking of refuge: the taking of refuge which is shared with practitioners of a lesser scope, the taking of refuge which is shared with practitioners of a medium scope, the taking of refuge which is shared with practitioners of a greater scope, cause refuge, and result refuge.
