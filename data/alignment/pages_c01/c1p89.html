GESHE MICHAEL'S ENGLISH (VERBATIM)
Right now it's absurd to say that these beings have any kind of pure happiness--they don't even have any of the impure kinds. They want no unwantables but don't want to know about giving up the bad deeds that bring pain. They act ass backwards: they do what they shouldn't and don't what they should. TIBETAN (WYLIE)

GESHE MICHAEL'S ENGLISH (VERBATIM)
"How good it would be if they could all find every happiness, and every cause of happiness. I wish they could. I'll see that they do." "How good it would be if they could all be free of every pain, and every cause of pain. I wish they could. I'll see that they do." Let these two trains of thought run through your mind; meditate on them over and over again. Then you will come to feel the very strongest loving-kindness and compassion.thams cad (#2)→within "every"ldan pa zhig→within "I'll see"bral ba zhig→within "I'll see"bskyed→within "come to feel"

Some people might come up with the idea that "Why should I take upon myself this great load, of every living being? There are plenty of Buddhas and bodhisattvas around to guide them on their way." This kind of thought though is absolutely improper. It's base. It's shameless. It's as if your mother in this life was hungry, and parched, and you expected someone else's child to go and give her food and drink.
