GESHE MICHAEL'S ENGLISH (VERBATIM)
And Chen-ngawa spoke in reply, "My master, leave alone any hope of realizing all the steps to Buddhahood--I would rather even to be a person who had just begun to get some glint of understanding, who could say to himself that he had started off on the first of these steps to Buddhahood.
"Why would this be my choice? In all my lives to now I've been a master of the five sciences, countless times. And countless times I've gained single-pointed concentration, even to where I could sit in meditation for an eon. The same with the five types of clairvoyance--and the eight great attainments. But never have I been able to go beyond the circle of life--never have I risen above it. If I were able to gain a realization of the steps to Buddhahood that Atisha taught, I would surely be able to turn from this round of births."
