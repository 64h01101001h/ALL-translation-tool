Segment 37 — verse 13: the exhortation

TIBETAN (WYLIE)
de ltar lam gyi gtso bo rnam gsum gyi, ,gnad rnams rang gi ji bzhin rtogs pa'i tshe, ,dben pa bsten te brtson 'grus stobs bskyed nas, ,gtan gyi mdun ma myur du sgrubs shig bu
GESHE MICHAEL'S ENGLISH (VERBATIM)
When you've grasped as well as I the essential points Of each of the three principal paths explained, Then go into isolation, my son, make mighty Efforts, and quickly win your ultimate wish.shig→imperative
AI NOTE — (1) INTERPRETIVE CALL, flagged: rang gi ji bzhin rtogs pa can read "when you realize them just as they are" (rang = yourself) or "as I myself realized them" (rang = the author). GMR's "as well as I" takes the second — a real commentarial choice, linked and left to the reviewer. (2) sgrubs shig — the imperative stem + Wilson's imperative particle shig — lands as "win"; and bu "son", the vocative Tsongkhapa aims at Ngawang Drakpa, becomes the tender "my son" mid-line. (3) gtan gyi mdun ma ("the eternal aim" — an old idiom) → "your ultimate wish"; layer 7: brtson 'grus = brtson "strive" + 'grus "diligence" → "Efforts". (4) His order: 2-1-3-4, the temporal clause fronted.
