TIBETAN (WYLIE)

GESHE MICHAEL'S ENGLISH (VERBATIM)
Now there is a great highway along which each and every Buddha of the three times travels. It is the single guiding lamp for living beings in all three lands.18 It is none other than the teaching known as the "Steps to Buddhahood." Segment 49 — the chain of necessity: four musts

TIBETAN (WYLIE)
de yang gzhan don du 'tshang rgya ba la sangs rgyas kyi go 'phang de bsgrub dgos, de bsgrub pa la sgrub tshul shes dgos, de la chos thos dgos, thos pa de yang lam ma nor ba zhig la byed dgos pa yin pas
GESHE MICHAEL'S ENGLISH (VERBATIM)
We would all like to become Buddhas so we could help others; but to do so, we have to work to achieve this state. To do this though we have to know how. And to know how, we must study the dharma. The study that we do, moreover, must center on a path that never errs.
