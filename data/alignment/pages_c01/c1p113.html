GESHE MICHAEL'S ENGLISH (VERBATIM)
If any of these things existed from its own side, it wouldn't have to rely on the group of parts we give its name, and then each one would have to exist out there, on its own. GESHE MICHAEL'S ENGLISH (VERBATIM)
We can take some local chieftain; he is chief only so far as we on our part call him "chief," and not out there from his own side. Segment 376 — the "me" in name only

GESHE MICHAEL'S ENGLISH (VERBATIM)
This "me" is the same way too. It is not something that exists out there on top of my body and mind.
