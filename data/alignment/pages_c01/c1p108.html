GESHE MICHAEL'S ENGLISH (VERBATIM)
The expression "all objects" refers to each and every object from basic physical matter on up to the omniscience of a Buddha. They all exist in dependence upon their parts, so in a manner of speaking their "cause and effect can never fail." The antithesis, which we seek to disprove, is that these objects could have the solid existence they appear to have: that they could exist naturally. "When they lose all solid appearance"--that is, when we perceive that there is not a single thing in the universe which has any true or natural existence--then we have found "the path that pleases the Buddhas."ces (quote marker)→"The expression"yin→copula

To cut this root, we must develop wisdom which perceives that no such "self" exists. If we were to discuss what no-self is in any detailed way, it would be best to apply a number of sections from the works on the Steps to the path; one example would be the "fourfold analysis."162 Here though we will give only a brief presentation of the most vital points concerning correct view, and we will use the classical reasoning based on interdependence.gsungs pa ltar ("as taught in")→folded into the citation

Segment 361 — the "me" analysis

TIBETAN (WYLIE)
de yang chos thams cad dgags gzhi dang 'dogs byed la brten nas byung ba las, de la ma ltos par rang ngos nas grub pa rdul tsam yang med pas, nga yang lus sems gnyis tshogs kyi steng du phar btags pa tsam la brten nas byung ba las, lus sems gnyis tshogs pa'i steng du phar btags pa la ma ltos par nga kho rang gi ngos nas tshur grub pa med, lus sems gnyis re re nas kyang rang rang gi ngos nas tshur grub pa med
GESHE MICHAEL'S ENGLISH (VERBATIM)
Now every existent object is a product of something to be given a name and something else to give it a name. There is not a single atom of anything in the universe which does not rely on this process--there is nothing which exists from its own side. I too then am a product: someone has taken two things together, my body and my mind, and called it "me." I am nothing more than that. There is no "me" which exists from its own side; there is no "me" which does not rely on someone taking my body and mind together and granting it the name. Neither in fact do my body or my mind themselves exist from their own sides.brten nas (#1)→within "a product of"grub pa→within "exists"
