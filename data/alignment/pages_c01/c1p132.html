TIBETAN (WYLIE)

GESHE MICHAEL'S ENGLISH (VERBATIM)
There are profound essential points even within these words the Lord has used about essential points. The word "isolation," for example, is meant to refer to isolating yourself not only on the outside--staying in some place far from the hustle and bustle of life--but within your own mind: keep your mind from making its usual intercontinental tour of the eight worldly thoughts and your thousand daily hopes and fears.172mang po ("many")→amplified into "thousand daily"yod de→"There are"zhes pa→"The word"de tsho las→"from" folded
