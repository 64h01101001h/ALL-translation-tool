GESHE MICHAEL'S ENGLISH (VERBATIM)
None of these things that seem to be pleasure though is pleasure by nature, or pleasure in its very essence. If they were, then you would feel more pleasant the more you had of them. But this is not the case, for as you get more and more of them they too start to give you pain. When this happens we can understand that they are not pleasure by nature. Segment 246 — the pervasive suffering

TIBETAN (WYLIE)

GESHE MICHAEL'S ENGLISH (VERBATIM)
The third type of suffering is known as the "pervasive suffering, which brings in more." The point here is that, regardless which one of the six kinds of birth we take, we take on a body which, by its very existence, comes complete with its own particular sufferings built in. From the first moment we take the various impure parts of our being on, from the first moment of their existence, they provide a basis for all the sufferings we have to look forward to in life: birth, aging, sickness, death, and all the rest. The impure parts of ourselves are like a big pot, sucking in the suffering of suffering, sucking in the suffering of change, in both this and our future lives. We must find a way to stop taking births, to stop taking on all the impure parts we're made of. Until we do, our existence will be like lying on a bed of upright needles--never a thing but pain.nyer len→within "we take on"grub (#2)→within "existence"tshe 'di→within "in life"zag bcas→within "impure"

Segment 247 — what escape really means

TIBETAN (WYLIE)
'khor ba las thar zhes pa yang lung pa gcig nas gzhan zhig tu thar ba lta bu la mi zer gyi, 'khor ba ni zag bcas nyer len gyi phung po'i rgyun nam cha de la bya bas, de bdag med rtogs pa'i shes rab kyis rtsa ba nas rgyun bcad pa na 'khor ba las thar ba zhes zer ba yin
GESHE MICHAEL'S ENGLISH (VERBATIM)
When we talk about "escaping cyclic life," it's not like running away from one country and managing to reach another. "Cyclic life" is precisely the continued existence of, the very fact of, the impure parts that make us up, the impure parts of our being that we've taken on. And when the continued existence of these parts is stopped at its root by the wisdom that realizes that nothing has a self-nature, this then is our "escape from cyclic life."gcig→within "one"zag bcas→within "impure"zhes zer→the coinage frame; quotes supplied
