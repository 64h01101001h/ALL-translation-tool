GESHE MICHAEL'S ENGLISH (VERBATIM)
But people didn't say it, because getting to be the chanting master depends on many other factors. We don't call someone "chanting master" until there is a basis to give the name--a monk who is fit to be chanting master, and until a person qualified to give him the name hangs it on him, and says "This is the chanting master." Neither until this time does the person himself think "I am the chanting master." But once the concept has been applied to him, "You are the chanting master," then people start to talk about him as "chanting master," and he too begins to think "I am the chanting master."dgos te→within "getting to be"phar→foldedde ltar→"But"kyang→"Neither" carries itzhes (#2)→the quote frame
