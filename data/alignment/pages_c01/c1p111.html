Segment 368 — this we call this

TIBETAN (WYLIE)

GESHE MICHAEL'S ENGLISH (VERBATIM)
And the same goes for every existing entity: they are nothing but a name and a concept, "This we call this, and that we call that," applied to the collection of parts that acts as the basis of the particular entity's name. Segment 370 — the three conditions

TIBETAN (WYLIE)
btags pa de'ang khyad chos gsum ldan zhig dgos, khyad chos gsum ni, tha snyad pa'i shes pa la grags pa yin pa dang , de la tha snyad pa'i tshad ma gzhan gyis mi gnod pa, don dam dpyod byed kyi rigs pas kyang mi gnod pa ste de gsum dang ldan dgos
GESHE MICHAEL'S ENGLISH (VERBATIM)
When we apply a label, three conditions must be present. The three are as follows: (1) the object must be known to a conventional perception; (2) no other conventional perception can contradict its existence; and (3) no ultimate analysis can contradict its existence either. All three must be there.ldan (#1)→within "must be present"ni→"as follows"yin pa→copuladpyod byed→within "analysis"
