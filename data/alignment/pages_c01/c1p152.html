Segment 491 — spears and canopies: two interdependences

TIBETAN (WYLIE)
rgyu las byung ba'i rten 'brel las kyang , lugs 'dir mdung khyim gyi tshul du rgyu mang 'bras bu la brten pas ltos nas btags pa'i rten 'brel gtso bor mdzad de
GESHE MICHAEL'S ENGLISH (VERBATIM)
Now the concept of interdependence can refer to the fact that things come from causes. Sometimes though things can be turned all upside down, like spears that you plant points-up to support a canopy, and we can say in many cases that "causes" depend on "results." Therefore the system that we use here is based principally on interdependence in the sense of labelling due to dependence.
AI NOTE — Gungtang distinguishes causal interdependence (rgyu las byung ba'i rten 'brel) from labelling-interdependence (ltos nas btags pa'i rten 'brel) — and the system here makes the LATTER principal. mdung khyim ("spear-house") → "like spears that you plant points-up to support a canopy"; "things can be turned all upside down" SUPPLIED as the explainer. rgyu mang splits: mang → "in many cases", rgyu → '"causes' (crossed). Clause e3 is a FOUR-UNIT MIRROR: Tibetan w11-w12-w13-w14 surfaces as English w14-w13-w12-w11. rten 'brel → "interdependence" both times (corpus consistency).

Segment 492 — the man named Tashi

TIBETAN (WYLIE)
btags pa'i tshul yang , sngar bkra shis mi zer ba'i mi gcig la rang gis bkra shis zhes btags nas zhag shas song ba na de la phar btags pa'i blo med par de'i ngos nas bkra shis grub pa 'dra zhig skye ba'i ngang tshul phra mo 'di rtogs dka' ste, glegs bam las nges nges 'dra ba na grub mtha' 'og ma tsho'i bdag med bshad pa'i don blo ngor ae 'dug mas nas bltas te mi 'dug na lta ba'i phyogs na med do,
GESHE MICHAEL'S ENGLISH (VERBATIM)
How does the labelling work? Suppose there's a person who never had the name "Tashi," and then one day you go and give him the new name "Tashi."208 After a few days you begin to forget that it was you who gave him the name "Tashi," and he starts looking like he is "Tashi" all on his own. The process by which your perception changes here is rather subtle and difficult to recognize. You can read all the books and start to think you know what no-self is, but then you've got to go back to the lower school systems and work your way up: make sure you can picture what they think "no-self" means as well. If you can't then you're probably pretty far from correct view yourself.209yang ("moreover")→the question frame "How does … work?"la (dative)→"to [a person]"de la ("to him")→absorbedlas ("from [the books]")→absorbed into "read"phar ("outward") — the DEIXIS word (cf. 377): the label went from YOU toward HIM; English carries the direction in "it was you who gave him"
AI NOTE — The famous naming-example (fn 208/209). bkra shis = "Tashi" ×3 in Tibetan; English re-supplies the name a 4th time ("forget that it was you who gave him the name 'Tashi'") — the extra stays unwrapped. phar (DEIXIS, cf. 377) nulled: the label travels outward from you. de'i ngos nas → "all on his own" — the Gelukpa term "from its own side" in street clothes. ngang tshul → "The process" (3rd rendering: way/manner/process). mas nas bltas te ("looking from below") → "go back to the lower school systems and work your way up"; ae 'dug → "make sure" — WYLIE QUEUE: 3rd ae token (cf. 436 ×2). nges nges reduplication ("start to think you know") joins grub grub (365) and gsungs gsungs (460). phyogs na med do → "you're probably pretty far from" — not even in the neighborhood of the view.

Segment 493 — understand this, understand that

TIBETAN (WYLIE)
don dam rtogs pa'i shes rab med na gzhan gnyis kyis mi thar ba bshad nas dngos la rten 'brel gsungs pa 'di go na go la, min na gnas lugs rtogs pa'i skabs med pa la dgongs
GESHE MICHAEL'S ENGLISH (VERBATIM)
Here in his verses Lord Tsongkapa first makes the statement that, unless you have the wisdom that perceives ultimate reality, the other two principal paths can never free you. Then the next thing he mentions, at least directly, is interdependence. The point he is trying to make is "If you understand interdependence, you understand ultimate reality; if not, then you don't."kyis (instrumental)→"by [the other two]"la (connective)→the semicolonrtogs pa'i (2nd, "realizing")→absorbed into "you understand"
