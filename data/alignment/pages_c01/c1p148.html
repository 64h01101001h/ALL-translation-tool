GESHE MICHAEL'S ENGLISH (VERBATIM)
Various records were made on the different occasions that he delivered this teaching; there were five such manuscripts in the hands of different people. I was able to complete this work because of the blessings of this highest of guides himself; those received during the many years I spent at his side while he was living, and those which even now emanate from his remains, his precious relics.
