GESHE MICHAEL'S ENGLISH (VERBATIM)
In short, if it comes down to choosing between giving up your life, and letting this morality degenerate, you had surely better choose the first. The one thing here in this birth that you cherish above all else is your own life. What you are requesting from your Lama is that he or she grant you the power to always keep your vows and pledges properly; that you reach a point where you cherish these commitments infinitely more than your own precious life.
