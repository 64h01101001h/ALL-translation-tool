AI NOTE — A verse citation in four lines; the mid-sentence capitals (Great, Drags, Downhill, Dispense, Of) are lineation, not apparatus, and And then he says, is Geshe Michael's prose frame with no Tibetan under it. Line 1 is a single locative noun phrase, dbyar sprin chen 'khrigs pa'i nam mkha' la = “in the sky where the great summer clouds are massed.” GMR breaks the relative clause into a main clause, so 'khrigs surfaces as the finite verb “crowd” while its nominalizer-genitive pa'i and the locative la go unrendered; the “In” of “In the summer” is his own, not this la. Note the swap: Tibetan sprin chen (noun, then adjective) becomes “Great clouds”. Line 2 has one glog; GMR supplies the second “And lightning” for the parallel, so only the first is wrapped, and both da lta (“right now … right now”) drop out entirely. Line 3: tshe mi rtag pa'i sdug bsngal = “the suffering of the impermanence of life”; GMR's “The suffering called impermanence … our life” splits tshe off and pushes it past the verb, which is why the English crosses here, and “called” is his rendering of the appositional genitive 'i, left unwrapped. The negation mi takes the “im” of “impermanence” and rtag pa the stem, per the campaign's negative-affix convention. dran is rendered “Drags”, i.e. read as 'dren “to pull” rather than dran “to recall”; the spelling is stable across all five stanzas here and again at C16:468–472, so it is the published reading. Line 4: rtag 'dzin (“holding to permanence”) is rendered as two crossing words, 'dzin → “sense” first and rtag → “permanence” second, with the hinge “Of” left to GMR. grabs shoms (“make preparations, be in readiness”) is the idiom behind “feeling prepared”; grabs (“readiness”) owns “prepared”, while “feeling” is the participial carrier for the verb shoms and is not banked as its gloss. kho thag … chod is the idiom “cut the matter through, settle it once and for all”, split by de nas; the noun half kho thag has no exponent, and the verb chod carries the whole phrasal verb “Dispense with” — “dispense” alone would mean “hand out”, which is not what chod says.
