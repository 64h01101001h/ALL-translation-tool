Segment 307 — Everything has come together — yet suppose we fail

GESHE MICHAEL'S ENGLISH (VERBATIM)
I will admit as well that everything we need has come together for us this time: we have a Lama, we have some Dharma to practice, we have every favorable condition that we need.
