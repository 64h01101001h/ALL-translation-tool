Segment 484 — the title of son of the buddha

TIBETAN (WYLIE)

GESHE MICHAEL'S ENGLISH (VERBATIM)
The minute we reach this state of mind, even if we have no other admirable qualities at all, we win the title of "Son (or Daughter) of the Buddha." Segment 486 — the shared paths and renunciation

TIBETAN (WYLIE)
de la skyes bu chung 'bring skabs ngan song dang 'khor ba'i sdug bsngal rang steng du bsgoms pas nges 'byung skyes yod tshe
GESHE MICHAEL'S ENGLISH (VERBATIM)
In the sections where we trained ourselves in the steps that are shared with practitioners of lesser and medium capacity, we meditated on the sufferings of the lower realms, and of the entire circle of life, but relative only to ourselves.
