THE UNEXPONENTED FRAME. gang la brten nas — four of the nine spans, roughly the first 40% of the wylie — carries no English at all. Read literally the heading is "the reasons depending on which one goes for refuge"; Geshe Michael compressed the whole relative frame into the bare head noun. All four spans are nulled rather than folded into "The Reasons". This is worth recording because the frame is usually rendered. C03 contains 13 instances of X la brten nas. Four are headings: 238 (de la brten nas -> "in which then"), 274 and 275 (gzhung la brten nas -> "found in the Books of"), and this one. 237 is the only heading in that family where the frame receives no English whatever, and it is the only C03 occurrence of gang la brten nas — the interrogative/relative variant.
