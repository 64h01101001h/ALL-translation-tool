THE GLOSS SIDE. phyin chad (lit. "cut off after," henceforth) → "in the future". yang yang "again and again" → "on a regular basis", idiomatic rather than literal — and the reduplication is why no member is drawn: yang occurs twice inside its own parent and is not uniquely placeable, which rule 4 forbids. mi rnyed takes "never be able to find", with mi → "never" and rnyed → "find" as depth-7 members; "be able to" is defensible as the attainment sense of rnyed and stays with the parent.

THE DUPLICATE rnyed, and a real trap. The wylie carries rnyed twice. The first is s2 at d=5.
