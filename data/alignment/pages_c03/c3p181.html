The English runs well past its Tibetan. GMR renders the single qualifier bsregs bcad brdar ba'i gser bzhin du dag ther legs par mdzad pa'i twice — compressed into “a truly pure system” and then expanded into the whole second sentence (“perfectly refined and assayed, like gold that has been through the fire, the shears, and the file”).
