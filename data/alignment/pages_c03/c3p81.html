WALKING THE TIBETAN. `thar pa` = liberation, the goal noun, here rendered "Nirvana". `don du gnyer ba` is the idiom "to strive after / seek out the aim of", taken whole: `don du` ("for the sake of") has no separate exponent, and `gnyer ba` alone would carry the same "Achieve" as the whole, so splitting buys nothing GMR's English distinguishes. The standalone particle `du` would be d=6 but carries no exponent, so it is omitted rather than mis-depthed — an omitted null costs nothing. `blo` = mind/attitude → "Wish". `bskyed pa` = to generate/develop → "Developing". The nominalizing `pa` in `thar pa` and `bskyed pa` carries nothing separable. The English inverts the Tibetan order completely (Developing / Wish / Achieve / Nirvana against thar pa / gnyer ba / blo / bskyed pa); rule 2 constrains the tib side only, so this is permitted.
