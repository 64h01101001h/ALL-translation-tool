The closing move of the argument from the lesser to the greater. The Tibetan runs bcas rgyang phra mo zhig gis kyang de ltar 'gyur na — ‘if even by a single minor precept it turns out like that’ — and GMR renders the concessive kyang not as a word but as the whole shape of the clause, if something so small as breaking a single minor vow can escalate so far.
