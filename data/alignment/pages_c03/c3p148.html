AI NOTE — Four verse lines, each closed by a shad; the initial capitals mid-sentence are lineation, not sentence starts. Parallel witness: the identical wylie and identical ACIP stand at C09:30, where the same verse is translated independently — “The entire teachings of the Victors reside in the three collections of scripture; Thus the essence of what they have taught can be found within the three trainings. Line 1 is one noun phrase, rgyal ba'i gsung rab ma lus, “all, without remainder, of the Victors' excellent speech”. GMR distributes it across three English phrases and adds an appositive, “The teaching of the Buddhas”, which duplicates rgyal ba'i gsung rab; only the genitive phrase is claimed there and “The teaching” is left unwrapped. gsung rab is split flat, rab → “highest” and gsung → “spoken words”, so that the supplied “of” between them is not captured. “Is contained in” is GMR's supplied verb — the Tibetan line has no verb at all — and stays unwrapped. de phyir (“therefore”) is likewise split flat, de → “This” and phyir → “why”: the “then is” between them is GMR's hinge and has no Tibetan under it. de dag is “these (plural)” and takes “These” whole; the “three” of “These three” is GMR's own supply (he does the same at C09:30, “all these three”), so it is not claimed.
