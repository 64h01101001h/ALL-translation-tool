# Source Trust Hierarchy — PROPOSED, for Geshe Michael's ratification
*Drafted 2026-08-14 at Adam's directive: "start an open dialog with
Geshe Michael about which sources to trust over others and why."
Everything below is how the tool ALREADY behaves; nothing here is
new practice. The purpose of the dialog is to have the authority
confirm, reorder, or strike — and to record his WHY beside each
tier, in his own words.*

## The standing rule above all tiers
**hgm_gloss is binding; everything else is reference.** Machine
work may MATCH Geshe Michael's English from corpus evidence, never
COMPOSE it. No lower tier is ever promoted by software; promotion
is a human act through the Propose → Approval lane.

## Tier I — The Authority (binding)
1. **Geshe Michael's published equivalents** (curated hgm_gloss).
2. **His glossary-sourced equivalents** (labeled tier).
3. **His live rulings** (the pronunciation/wording rulings lane —
   e.g. tsema namdrel; le'ur) — override every display.
4. **His recorded teachings** (the recordings are the authority;
   transcripts and caption indexes only LOCATE them).

*Open question for GMR: within Tier I, when a published gloss and
a recorded spoken gloss differ, which governs, and how should the
card show the other?*

## Tier II — Provisional-from-the-Authority (labeled, demoted)
5. **Auto-aligned provisional glosses** (matched from his corpus by
   machine; PROVISIONAL label, red).
6. **AI Working Glossary drafts** (violet block; wall-screened
   against his corpus; promotion only by human review).

## Tier III — Allied reference (licensed, labeled, never binding)
7. **Mahāvyutpatti** (the imperial standard — historically closest
   to a canonical reference).
8. **84000 glossary** (CC BY; institutional, contemporary).
9. **Monlam word lists** (attestation only — a word EXISTS; says
   nothing about HIS equivalent).
10. **Lokesh Chandra** (Sanskrit equivalences; 92.2% verified lane).

*Open questions for GMR: relative order of 7–10; should 84000
renderings ever appear beside his equivalents on the same line, or
always in the reference zone?*

## Tier IV — Historical reference (PD scans/texts; labeled)
11. **Das 1902** (page-scan links; headword-level, ±page).
12. **Jäschke 1881** (page-scan links; letter-level).
13. **eKangyur/eTengyur e-canon** (witness readings in collation —
    never auto-corrections).

## Tier V — Local-only compilations (unlicensed; never shipped)
14. **Hopkins, {C}, {PH}, {GD}, OT, IW, TD, THL stacks** — local
    lookup convenience only; excluded from all release data.

*Open question for GMR: any he would strike entirely from view?*
