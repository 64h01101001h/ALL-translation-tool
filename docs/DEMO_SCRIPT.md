# The 15-Minute Demo — script for tonight

> *Preserved as delivered 2026-08-07. Tab paths predate the six-group
> navigation of 2026-08-10; see `data/help/tutorials.md` for current
> paths.*

*For Adam, presenting to ~550 students and sponsors at Geshe Michael's
request. Arc: from the preserved library, to the trained translator,
to the master's living voice. Every click below is rehearsable.*

## Pre-flight (30 minutes before, on the demo machine)

1. Launch the app once beforehand (dock icon appears; first-launch
   jitters happen offstage). If using the DMG on another Mac:
   right-click → Open the first time.
2. **Pre-open these so tabs are warm:**
   - Library pane → find **S0134** (the Mind-Training text) — you'll
     open it live, but know where it is.
   - Overlay → open **KD0016** (Diamond Cutter) → press **"Follow
     along in scans"** NOW to confirm the BDRC images load on the
     venue's network. If they don't: skip step 4 below gracefully —
     everything else is offline.
3. Display: app window maximized; macOS text size legible from the
   back — the Overlay's own font sizes are fine on a projector.
4. Have the phone gallery QR/link ready to show at the close (the
   artifact page). Optional: AirDrop the PANE_GALLERY.docx to a
   colleague as backup.
5. Breathe. 38 test suites and a 62-check self-test passed today;
   the software is the calmest thing in the room.

## The script (minute by minute)

**0:00 — The opening line.**
> "Forty years ago, ACIP began typing a civilization's library,
> keystroke by keystroke. Tonight I want to show you what that
> library just became."
Launch from the Dock (gold ALL on maroon). One sweep of the fifteen
tabs and the menu bar: "Fifteen working areas, one shared dictionary
— and everything runs offline, so this works in a monastery in Nepal
exactly as it works here."

**1:00 — The Overlay: reading with the dictionary underneath (3 min).**
Library pane → double-click S0134 → it opens in the Overlay, every
known word shaded in ACIP.
- "Every shaded phrase is a dictionary hit — 105,634 entries,
  anchored to Geshe Michael's own English."
- Display → **Tibetan script** (the wow: same shading, native script).
- **Click one word** (sems can): the card — tier label, *the
  concordance of Geshe-la's actual translations, course by course*,
  honorific badge, references honestly marked as references.
- Line to land: "The machine never invents English. It only ever
  shows you what the master already said."

**4:00 — The woodblock (1 min, network permitting).**
Overlay with KD0016 → "Follow along in scans" → pages turn with the
cursor. "The student sits with the digital text and the actual
carving of it — anywhere on earth." *(If network fails: one sentence
— "and when we're online, the original woodblock follows your cursor
— " and move on.)*

**5:00 — The Trainer: learning to read (2 min).**
Trainer → paste the line you prepared (or SEMS CAN THAMS CAD BDE BA
DANG LDAN PAR GYUR CIG) → reveal layers one by one: boundaries →
particle roles → *find the verb first* → vocabulary → **the answer
key: Geshe-la's own English from the corpus** → the full Wilson
parse. "No other software on earth renders this parse. And the only
'answers' it ever shows are his."

**7:00 — The Draft bench + Review (2 min).**
Draft → Load source (same text) → click clause 1 → **the color-linked
anchors**: the Tibetan phrase and its English glosses share a hue.
Click **Phrase memory** on a selection: "every time Geshe-la has ever
rendered these words, instantly." Then Review pane, one sentence:
"and when a draft is done, a senior translator gets register
warnings, honorific advisories — guidance, never verdicts."

**9:00 — Gofer (2 min — the heart moment).**
Search tab. Pause.
> "Some of you know Geshe-la searches his library every single day
> with a tool called Gofer. This IS Gofer — the same search
> algorithms, rebuilt inside, pointed at everything."
Type two terms (e.g. "sdug bsngal" / "bden pa"), combiner NEAR,
proximity 5 → Find → hits across the aligned corpus with his English
beside the Tibetan. Show the Add button: "and it searches any folder
you give it, exactly as his Gofer always has."

**11:00 — Convert + the scripts (1.5 min).**
Convert → type *pramāṇa* → the cascade: IAST, three ACIP Sanskrit
styles, Tibetan script, Devanagari, Whitney's root analysis. "Proven
against 26,000 ground-truth pairs at 99%. And—" click **Sanskrit
OCR** if set up, else say — "it now reads Devanagari from images,
as it reads Tibetan woodblocks."

**12:30 — The living community: Propose & Approve (2 min — the close
for GMR).**
Propose tab, one glance. Then **Approval tab**:
> "Every member of our team can propose — a pronunciation, an
> honorific, a rendering. And every proposal comes here, to the
> authority. Tonight there are 225 items waiting: 205 pronunciations
> our engines derived from Geshe-la's own teaching — the first one
> is *kamdir*, his own example — and 20 honorifics mined this
> weekend from four grammars, every one carrying its citation."
Filter → HIGH honorifics → show *chibs bsgyur gnang*. "When Geshe-la
approves an item, it becomes the app — for every student, forever.
His judgment is the binding layer. That is the whole design."

**14:30 — Close (30 sec).**
> "Fifteen panes, one principle: the machine may match the master's
> English — it may never compose it. It runs offline, it self-tests
> — 62 checks on any machine — and it's ready for our translators
> now."
Show the phone-gallery QR/link. "The full tour is on this page —
and the technical overview is in your hands whenever you want it."

## Fallbacks

- **BDRC offline** → skip step 4 with the one-liner; nothing else
  needs network.
- **A pane misbehaves** → the menu bar reaches every function;
  switching tabs and back re-renders.
- **Time runs short** → cut Convert (11:00) first, then Draft's
  Review sentence; never cut Gofer or Approval.
- **Time runs long / Q&A** → the Drills pane and Input pane (ACE
  reborn — type beside the scan) are the best encores.
