**The rule as stated (via Adam, 2026-08-08): SKABS 'DIR is pronounced
"kamdir"** — a syllable-final -b(s) becomes **m** before a following
'a-chung syllable, keeping its own labial place regardless of what
follows (m before a dental in kamdir; m before a velar in the
dictionary's own attested kyamdro).

Everything below is DERIVED by applying that rule mechanically — each
juncture located by prefix pronunciation through the canonical engine,
only word-internal junctures transformed (the sandhi does not apply
across word boundaries, as the dictionary's own pronunciations show),
and no derived form contradicts any attested pronunciation. Nothing
here enters the pronunciation convention without approval; today the
forms only widen what the Lookup search accepts, labeled "derived".

**To approve**: strike any rows that are wrong; anything unstruck can
