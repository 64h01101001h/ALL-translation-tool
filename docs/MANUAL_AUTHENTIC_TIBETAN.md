# Manual of Authentic Tibetan — brought in, verified, mined (2026-08-09)

Source: https://freetibet.org/wp-content/uploads/2022/08/Manual-of-Authentic-Tibetan.pdf
(19.8 MB, 172 pp, clean text layer; PDF created 2018).

## What it is

*Manual of Authentic Tibetan* (བོད་སྐད་དག་མའི་སློབ་དེབ།), **Tenzin
Choephel** with Klara Hendrychova, © **Thosamling Nunnery** (Institute
for International Buddhist Women, Sidhpur/Dharamsala), first published
2018. A beginner's textbook of **modern colloquial (Lhasa) Tibetan**:
alphabet, tones, two units of graded lessons, honorific usage marked
"(H)" throughout, cultural notes.

## Trust verdict: TRUSTED for modern colloquial facts

- **Author**: native-speaker Tibetan teacher at Thosamling; the
  acknowledgements note his continuing studies at Oxford.
- **Endorsement**: back-cover blurb by **Ulrike Roesler, Professor of
  Tibetan and Himalayan Studies, University of Oxford** — a top
  academic authority.
- **Proofread** by Ngawang Tsundue of Sarah College (the Tibetan
  teacher-training college).
- **Spot-checks pass**: every (H) item checked matches known zhe-sa
  usage (khong, phebs, mtshan, mjal, 'khrungs, gsung, sku compounds);
  the dbu → [wu] prefix-pronunciation rule is correct.
- **Distribution**: hosted publicly by Free Tibet; the book carries a
  standard rights notice, so we hold it as an in-house reference and
  take facts with citation only.

**Register caveat (important):** this is MODERN SPOKEN Tibetan. The
honorific *vocabulary* transfers to the classical register and is what
we mine; the **tonal pronunciations do NOT map to the GMR
convention** and were deliberately not touched.

## What was mined

- `data/manual_authentic/honorific_findings.tsv` — every (H)-marked
  item curated into pairs. **Three new register candidates seeded as
  proposals** (queue now 214): **khong** (honorific he/she, p.39),
  **mtshan** (name, p.46), **mjal** (to meet, p.46 — now carrying TWO
  independent citations, this manual + Hackett p.156). Five items were
  already in the register (phebs, bzhugs, 'khrungs, gsung, sku); five
  honorific compounds banked as usage evidence (sku gzugs, sku khams,
  sku par, 'khrungs skar, sku srung).
- Grammar notes recorded: the polite particle ལགས་ with copulas; the
  two-tier polite/plain pronoun system.
- Full text extraction banked at
  `data/manual_authentic/manual_text_extraction.txt` (in-house
  research aid, same footing as the Hackett OCR — never shipped).
