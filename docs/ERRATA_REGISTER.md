## 1. What this is

Defects found in the ALL core documents &mdash; the Tibetan (ACIP/Wylie) source and Geshe Michael Roach's facing English &mdash; recorded as a by-product of a machine alignment campaign reading the courses segment by segment. **Every entry has been checked against the corpus database** (42,199 segments, spine `hgm_spine_v27_2.db`): the quoted strings were retrieved from the spine, not from a note, and each citation was re-derived rather than trusted.

**175 entries**: **127 document errata** (6 HIGH, 71 MEDIUM, 50 LOW), **24 digitisation artefacts**, 14 observations checked and closed as *not* errata, and 1 that could not be verified.

## 2. What we are NOT claiming

- **Nothing has been changed.** No document, ACIP file, English text or database row has been edited on the strength of this register.
- **Geshe Michael's English is preserved byte-for-byte** by standing project rule. Every "should say" below is a proposal for a human editor, not something the project has adopted.
- **Confidence means what it says.** CONFIRMED was reproduced in the spine with named witnesses. PROBABLE and UNCERTAIN need a human decision and must not be actioned automatically.
- **This is not a systematic proofread.** It records anomalies the campaign happened to notice while aligning. **An unflagged segment is not a clean segment; it is an unexamined one.**
- **Coverage is partial.** C01 and C02 in full, C03 in progress, C04&ndash;C18 unread at this depth &mdash; though duplicate passages inside them are named where a fix reaches them.
